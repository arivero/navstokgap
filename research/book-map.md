The integrated Planck manuscript retains its source-based historical exposition;
technical canonical notes hold the reusable proofs and their exact domains.
Independent oscillator, spin, transport and regulator manuscripts retain their
own constructions and source evidence; [manuscript classifications](pdf-audit.md)
identify their current or historical use. Failed routes belong beside the
claim they restrict, with [obstructions](obstructions.md) supplying retrieval.
