# Choose work by the conclusion it can change

Autonomous research should advance the necessity of quantum structure with a
positive universal action parameter, or a gap estimate that survives the
relevant limits. A solvable continuation is useful when it changes one of
those conclusions. The current ordered work list lives only in
[STATE](STATE.md); [TASKS](TASKS.md) records scope, acceptance and status.
Explicit user requests select their own scope.

## Research before serial verification

User correction, 2026-09-14: too many audits are drifting from the research.
The coordinator is a researcher and must also generate new ideas. Propose
candidate mechanisms, compare their physical motivation and decisive tests,
and develop the strongest candidate far enough to obtain a calculation,
construction or informative failure. Label conjectures explicitly; uncertainty
is a reason to investigate, not to retreat into another source audit.
A chain of named lemmas in a borrowed theorem is not enough to justify a chain
of autonomous sessions. Use established results with their assumptions and
recorded evidence status. Make construction, physical-premise testing and
surviving gap estimates the deliverables. Source review supports that work;
it does not replace it. Reopen a borrowed proof for a concrete suspected error
that affects a result in use, or explicit user direction. New mathematical
results retain the required bounded literature review; do not turn that review
into an automatic successor research task.

## Before starting a bounded task

Record a short decision in the handoff, then proceed without asking for routine
approval:

- **Target:** the particular quantum/action or gap proof obligation.
- **Decision:** which candidate premise or mechanism will be retained,
  rejected or revised under each likely outcome.
- **Deliverable and stop:** the theorem, counterexample, dependency map or
  source passage that finishes this step; include the source budget if needed.
- **Why now:** why existing results do not already settle this decision.

For action selection, name the observable, preparation/model class and limit;
locate the physical input that could exclude zero. Check the
[dimensional criteria](../notes/action-unit-dimensional-selection.md) first:
the fixed constants must admit a mass-independent action product, and no
admitted similarity may rescale the observable. For gap work, name the
operator, physical or auxiliary clock and constants that must survive size,
cutoff or coupling limits. A reference to the broad project aim alone does
not explain the value of another coefficient calculation. A supporting lemma
qualifies when it discharges a named dependency of the decisive deliverable;
identify that dependency and the result it enables.

## When a branch has become too specialized

A new pulse choice, extra calibration, sharper coefficient or additional solvable
example does not automatically create a successor task. First test whether its
possible outcomes change the central decision. If both leave the same missing
physical premise and discharge no named dependency, consolidate the result and
park the extension with its exact open question and a criterion for returning to it.

After two consecutive bounded steps in the same model or proof family, compare
its next step with the other main track and the existing supporting tasks.
Continue only with a written reason that the new step can change a main
conclusion or finish a previously named decisive deliverable. This is a
coordinator decision, not a new permission requirement. Corrections of an
accepted proof remain necessary work; preserve the user's explicit task choice.

A blocked source route should end at its stated budget, with coverage and the
remaining dependency recorded. Another available main-track task can proceed.
Do not expand a reading campaign merely because more related sources exist.
Lawful public source alternatives within the assigned scope and budget need no
renewed user permission. Workers cannot enlarge their own budgets; the
coordinator can select another bounded task under existing authorization.
Separate direction remains required for external correspondence, paid services
and the prohibited computational-verification workflow.

## Finish by reconciling priorities

A handoff states the result, remaining uncertainty and strategic consequence.
Label a suggested continuation as selected, supporting or parked. Update the
ordered STATE list; local notes and older handoffs may preserve technical
follow-ups but do not dispatch work. Keep STATE short and move dated milestone
narrative to history. PROGRAMME explains the questions and evidence, not a
second queue. Source-idea cards provide inputs, not scheduling instructions.

Existing proofs and audits survive consolidation. A synthesis that only
restates accepted claims needs a claim-to-source map and written consistency
review; it need not commission duplicate librarian work. New claims being
promoted receive the bounded proof and literature review required by AGENTS.md;
exploratory ideas need no prior audit.
