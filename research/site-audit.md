The old homepage used recency-like accumulated tracks rather than a minimal
dependency graph. The new map retains low-dimensional gaps and historical
source work through explicit support links without presenting every side
result as an immediate prerequisite. Public wording is checked against the
current source notes, not their PDF titles alone.

The ledger's historical evidence entries and stable IDs are not rewritten.
Its C001 “symbolic script” evidence label is historical support, as the ledger
header explains; it is not current validation under the no-scripts rule.
A public banner points readers to current mark/disturbance and correction
sources; imported citations keep their original reading labels. The known
factor inconsistency in flow-instability-large-field is flagged in the catalog
and obstruction map, and exact saturation is not promoted on the homepage.

A bounded Astra architecture review checked the four sensitive frontiers.
Its corrections are integrated: graph arrows label routing rather than
implying every edge is a proved input, packet stability names its supplied
quantum reference, disturbance spreads name the interpolating comparison
states, and the supporting cutoff Feshbach calculation retains its SU(2)
scope before an SU(3) transfer.

The tutorial retains its twelve-slide visual devices and corrects their
scope; its ending table links current sources individually. Series/parallel
and the Gaussian precision analogy stay useful, while the full nonabelian
iteration is explicitly open. See [navigation test](navigation-test.md) for
the final reader paths and [PDF audit](pdf-audit.md) for manuscript handling.
