## Changes tested by the new navigation

- The homepage is a research map with results, open fronts, corrections and
  two short branches. The complete archive is a separate filterable page.
- Each scientific note has one archive entry, with retrieval priority,
  status, keywords and explicit correction redirects where relevant.
- Every PDF has a classified entry, including eight manuscripts without a
  matching note. PDF titles do not override the linked version/scope caveat.
- Dependency, obstruction, notation, analogy, book and programme maps are
  readable as generated HTML and linked from the public map.
- The mandatory local retrieval skill searches full notes, ideas, sources,
  references and historical manuscripts as well as the compact catalog.
  A high-value negative note stays retrievable even when its title says failure.
- The catalog has a coverage/metadata check in make check; adding a maintained
  note without updating the catalog fails the integrity gate.

The current large gauge and recording notebooks remain long. Section-level
frontier links and dependency instructions reduce irrelevant reading while
preserving their derivations. The old ledger and apparatus compilation are
valuable specialist resources, but too broad or too old to serve as the
default current-status entry point. No new scientific synthesis is required
to reach either current frontier; the refinement synthesis described in
[synthesis opportunities](synthesis-opportunities.md) remains a useful future
book chapter rather than an additional research task.
