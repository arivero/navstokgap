for C-C_*=alpha L e_y, abs(alpha)<=epsilon, the exact reference-record
curve has canonical projection lambda s squared beta+O(lambda squared abs(s)),
where beta=-Pi L^{-1}V(e_y). Signed-coupling symmetry proves D V(0)=0;
thus the previously proposed first tangent test vanishes in both components.
The explicit quadratic pulse integrals (13) include moving-reference
subtraction, all four probes and the first-momentum squares in apparatus energy.

With S=min(s_0,epsilon/lambda), the local canonical risk product is bounded
by a fixed action constant times (lambda S squared+lambda squared S) squared.
If both beta entries are nonzero and S/lambda is sufficiently large, a
centre-to-endpoint comparison gives order lambda squared S to the fourth.
Neither beta nonvanishing nor exact canonical constancy is established.
The pulses, box, units and receiver ball stay fixed. These are conditional
radii on one compact local fibre, not full-class minimax upper bounds.
