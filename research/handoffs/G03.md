## Completed result and review

C124 establishes gap(-Q_N)=a[1-tanh(2b)] for every N>=3. The conditional Gibbs
refresh law is reversible and irreducible at finite b. Common site clocks and
uniform random numbers contract expected Hamming distance at that rate; the
induced Lipschitz contraction bounds every nonconstant eigenvalue. Total
magnetization attains it. Mean-velocity susceptibility divided by its variance
is exactly the inverse gap. At b=0 this recovers G02 with flip rate a/2.

At bounded b and a positive per-site clock floor, the bound survives increasing
finite volume. The explicit b_N=(log N)/4 family has gap 2a/(N+1); normalizing
the total refresh rate instead gives the separate 1/N slowdown. Thus finite
range alone is insufficient in joint limits. No infinite-volume operator,
physical quantum Hamiltonian or universal action parameter is supplied.
