## Findings

Definition 2 (printed p. 12) defines turns as interior orthogonal step changes
and the amplitude normalization
`(1+m^2 eps^2)^((1-t/eps)/2) i sum_s (-i m eps)^turns`, with
`P=|a|^2`, natural units `hbar=c=1`. Proposition 5 (printed p. 13,
equations (3)–(4)) is the two-component recurrence: `a1` takes the
`x+eps` predecessor and `+m eps a2`; `a2` takes `x-eps` and `-m eps a1`,
both divided by `sqrt(1+m^2 eps^2)`. Proposition 6 (same page) states exact
sum-over-x probability one for every positive lattice time. Its proof is at
printed/PDF p. 31 (p. 30 is the dependency chart and was not visually
inspected); it is a direct sum-of-squares cancellation. Thus the remembered recurrence
number is 5, not 1.

Corollary 5 (p. 17, (22)) gives a Feynman triple limit to the non-relativistic
free kernel under explicit sequence conditions. Corollary 6 (p. 18, (23)) gives
the compact-uniform continuum limit to the spin-1/2 retarded Dirac propagator.
The source explains the `1/(2 eps)` normalization. On p. 19 it warns that
light-cone delta terms make the singular point-source propagator square
ill-defined, while regular L2 wavepackets retain unitary norm conservation;
finite-step probability conservation has no direct point-source/global-charge
analogue.

## Candidate construction audit

The proposed real coin `(1-lambda eps)I+lambda eps sigma_x` with shifted
components is stochastic for `0 <= lambda eps <= 1` and conserves l1
probability, but is not generally l2-unitary. The proposed complex coin
`C=(I-i omega eps sigma_x)/sqrt(1+omega^2 eps^2)` is exactly unitary;
translations `f_+(x-c eps), f_-(x+c eps)` are unitary, so `U=SC` is exactly
unitary at finite step. Its first-order expansion yields
`iK dt psi=(-iKc sigma_z dx+mc^2 sigma_x)psi` for `omega=mc^2/K`, `K>0`,
with basis/order as recorded in the companion (coin-after-shift differs only at
O(eps^2) in the first-order generator; reversing shift flips transport sign).
The dispersion and
non-relativistic expansion, fixed-T Fourier product limit, and real telegraph
`D=K/(2m)` are derived coordinator consequences, not source claims. Real
stochastic analytic continuation requires a global phase and is not finite-step
unitary. The physical premise selecting complex amplitudes and selecting `K`
remains open.

## C040 addendum

An ideal projective direction measurement after every complex-coin step,
starting from a definite direction, has exact Born flip probability
`q=omega^2 eps^2/(1+omega^2 eps^2)`. For `N=T/eps`,
`P(any flip) <= Nq <= omega^2 T eps -> 0`. Hence fixed omega yields a
ballistic continuous-cut limit, distinct from finite-rate telegraph scaling
`q=lambda eps`. This is an elementary measurement-protocol consequence of
the audited coin, not a novelty claim.
