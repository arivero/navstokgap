## Mathematical review

The note's capacity proof is valid: each local affine effect is at most twice
its value at the zero state; tensoring gives the (2^n) bound, and normalization
at the zero reference state yields (k\le2^n). Product opposite-pole tests
attain equality. The convex-hull extreme-point argument makes all pure
composites product-pure, so a mixed local zero state has no purification.
The equal-sign face is a two-point-capacity line segment, not a ball, and hence
fails the intended Hardy subspace geometry.

## Strategic consequence

Retain closure and local reversibility as useful requirements, but reject them
as a sufficient quantum selector. Q01 must choose an independent physical
composition premise—globally pure/entangled states, purification, or a
subspace principle. The source supplies neither that mechanical premise nor a
dimensional action constant, phase law, or action floor. No novelty assertion.
