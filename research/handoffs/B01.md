## Findings for coordinator integration

- The three reconstructions are alternative axiom systems, not one interchangeable proof. Hardy explicitly identifies continuity as the premise that excludes the classical probability model. Chiribella et al. use an operational-probabilistic framework with purification selecting quantum theory. Masanes–Müller use five physical requirements and discuss generalized state spaces and possible modifications.
- Feynman’s primary abstract explicitly calls the action-phase rule a postulate: a path contribution has phase given by the classical action in units of $\hbar$. This supports treating $e^{iS/\hbar}$ as admitted quantum input (Branch A), not as a consequence of Newtonian trajectory limits.
- Wootters gives a finite-sample operational starting point: with a fixed finite number of systems, statistical fluctuations limit distinguishability. Audenaert et al. give an asymptotic many-copy optimal error exponent. Neither source supplies the project’s path encoding, resource budget, error criterion, or action-to-state map.
