## Result and consequence

[The note](../../notes/low-dimensional-mass-gap.md) proves Theorem 1 (a
floor of any observable is a dimensionless multiple of a unit formed from
the fixed constants, else zero or infinite), Theorem 3 (exact scaling
$\varepsilon=\hbar^{2k/(k+2)}\lambda^{2/(k+2)}m^{-k/(k+2)}$), Theorems 5--6
(the $x^2y^2$ and SU(2) matrix models have compact resolvent, simple ground
state and gap $\delta_1\hbar^{4/3}g^{2/3}m^{-2/3}$; the gap vanishes as
$\hbar\to0$ and as $g\to0$; the abelian model is free; $D=1$ has no
potential), Proposition 7 (zero-mode gap $\delta_1^{(3)}g^{2/3}\hbar c/L$ on
the torus, positive for every $L$ and decaying as $1/L$), Propositions 8--10
(free fields inherit the classical dispersion; dimensional census over
$d=2,3,4$), Proposition 11 (two-dimensional Yang--Mills on a circle:
$E_R=\tfrac12g^2\hbar^2cL\,C_2(R)$, diverging with $L$), Propositions 12--13
(two-dimensional delta and inverse-square potentials: a scale forced by the
definition of the dynamics, without a field-theoretic gap) and Proposition
14 (the constant-force Galileo class is similarity-covariant and has floor
zero; $(m,F)$ admit no action unit).

Strategic consequence: the gap track has a written gate, supplied versus
generated; C124/C126 and G05 are supplied gaps, and G06 would be. The
analogy is exact for the structure-to-scale stage of STATE item 1 and is a
labelled heuristic for the consistency-to-structure stage; its one
consistency-forced scale (self-adjoint extension, regularization) is what
N02 should look for, tested against C027 and C028--C029. The conjecture of
a similarity anomaly is labelled as such in the note. No ledger claim is
promoted; promotion would need written proof review and one bounded
librarian comparison.
