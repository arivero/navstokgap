## Result

$\ell(1-\cos z)/(\gamma z)$; the phase-averaged position-conditioned coefficient
is $\ell\sin^2z/(2\gamma z)$. Both vanish at short/long windows. A fixed
positive fraction of orbital action bounds the window endpoint ratio.
Twice the centered canonical covariance area equals $\ell$. The fixed-orbit
symplectic map conserves the determinant for every phase law, including zero
for point phase. The Kepler threshold transfers within uniform preparation;
softening the centre closes it. This defines a classical constant observable
and identifies preparation/scale selection as the remaining physical task.
