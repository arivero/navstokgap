## Findings

The orbit proof is valid: complexification, restriction to the explicit curve, analytic exponentials, and the distinct-time Vandermonde determinant. The expectation lemma is valid by a finite signed atomic relation among evaluation vectors; inclusion of 1 gives equal masses and normalization to probability preparations. Nonlinear expectation updates cannot evade it while working for every preparation.
