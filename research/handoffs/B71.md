## Findings and evidence boundaries

- Detailed balance gives the symmetric square-root transform. The sources use
  a probability-column transition matrix; C126 uses a row generator on
  functions. Transposition reconciles Henley's `P^(-1/2) W P^(1/2)` with
  C126's `D^(1/2) Q D^(-1/2)`.
- The sources establish the square-root Boltzmann zero mode, positive local
  blocks, spectral correspondence and locality inherited from local moves.
- Castelnovo et al. explicitly leave the symmetric move coefficients free and
  list Glauber dynamics as one choice. This supports the clock-dependence
  conclusion.
- The source's `K=beta J` is dimensionless. C126's action-valued `K` is a new
  input and must not be attributed to that notation.
- The explicit three-site Ising heat-bath Pauli coefficients and the exact
  all-mode gap were not found verbatim. They are correct project derivations
  from C124 under the manual audit, not sourced claims.
