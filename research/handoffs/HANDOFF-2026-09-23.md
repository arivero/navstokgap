## How the user works

  When a claim fails, restate it as the true theorem with its hypothesis
  explicit, and keep the correction dated.
- $\hbar$ and $c$ explicit everywhere; this repository's $g^2$ already
  contains $\hbar$ ($g^2=\hbar g_{\rm cl}^2$).

  is. A short load-bearing contrast is allowed.

  companion) before it enters a note; label reading levels.
