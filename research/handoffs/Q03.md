# Q03: can a finite energy depot select persistent action?

2026-09-14. Exploratory mechanism test; no accepted-ledger promotion.

## Decision before derivation

Test ACTION_FIELD_TARGET obligations 2--4 with an autonomous nonlinear
oscillator/depot system. Compare finite stored fuel with constant replenishment.
Retain the mechanism if it attracts positive action without supplying a
positive external flux or a target action in its coefficients. Stop at an
energy balance and an explicit attractor test, rather than a general survey.

## Result and strategic consequence

[The construction](../../notes/energy-depot-action-selection.md) gives an
exact transfer balance and a Lyapunov function proving attraction of every
positive seed above pumping threshold. Finite fuel decays exponentially;
the pumped action depends on supplied power and inverse frequency. Thus
nonlinear amplitude stabilization succeeds but universal action selection
fails in this model. The exact zero seed also remains invariant.

operator identification, and another auxiliary parent would leave that
unchanged. Original Q02 files were preserved, neither edited nor staged.
