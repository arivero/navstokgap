# M04: source-driven time refinement

The free-particle experiment now has a readable paper, exact algebra checks and
a per-result literature/assumption audit. Its main contribution to the programme
is a precise selection question: temporal refinement preserves an arbitrary
action parameter, so the physical premise selecting a positive universal value
must be identified separately.

## Source verification and review

H04 used one GPT-5.6 Luna worker at medium effort. After its completion and
coordinator review, B06 used one GPT-5.6 Luna worker at medium effort. The
coordinator waited during each run; no descendants or parallel agent work.
Effective runtime configurations were not independently reported.

Coordinator read Rivero's three- and five-page notes, selected Brouder and
tangent-groupoid introductory passages, and the source passages cited in B06.
The new proofs were checked directly; B06 supplied a bounded assumption audit.
This remains internal review with symbolic checks, rather than formal verification.

Corrections retained in companions: e-rara image indices differ from printed
pages; Teschl is the 2009 first edition; Pitman–Yor covariance is printed p. 6;
the second equality in their p. 10 bridge display omits a factor 1/T. The correct
bridge formula is independently derived in the paper. Historical ownership,
invocation and influence are separate evidence questions.

## Restart: M05 with B07

Start from the existing normalized finite Gaussian kernels. Write the joint
endpoint-conditioned law at an arbitrary finite partition, then its covariance,
Hessian determinant and normalization. Use this to test Rivero's two-regulator
proposal on a quadratic action. Read the cited Wilson–Kogut chapter 12 only to
extract the control map needed by that calculation. Separately extract the
tangent-groupoid theorem's hypotheses and limit topology for the strong
quantum-completion target. Every source reading should supply a concrete test.

Keep mesh refinement, action-scale limits, state-space limits and c^{-1}
separate. The positive Gaussian bridge and unitary quantum evolution are
distinct constructions; their physical connection is a proof obligation.

## Reproduction

Run `make check` and `make papers`. The latter builds foundations, time
refinement and programme PDFs with resolved references and no layout overflow.
The programme source is Markdown; its TeX is generated. Original research PDFs
whose redistribution permission is unresolved remain in ignored `.build/`;
the published repository contains their metadata, links and companions.
