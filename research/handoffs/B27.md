## Result and exact source coverage

Boyer (2004), arXiv `physics/0405090v1`, directly classifies the three regimes
around \(L=\alpha/c\). Exactly arXiv-PDF pp. 5, 6, 9, and 10 were read.
Page 6, Eqs. (19)--(20), gives the turning polynomial and roots. Pages 9--10,
Eqs. (37)--(42), give the supercritical, endpoint, and subcritical orbit forms,
identify two-turning-point bound rosettes only for \(L>\alpha/c\), and verify
finite-time arrival at the centre when \(L\leq\alpha/c\). Three discovery
queries were used; one source was retained. Search/HTML routing was not counted
as PDF-page reading.

For
\(H=\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2(p_r^2+L^2/r^2)}-k/r\), a written effective-potential
derivation gives the unique well bottom
\(mc^2\sqrt{1-(k/(c|L|))^2}\) when \(|L|>k/c\). At equality the infimum zero
lies only at \(r=0\); below equality the effective potential is unbounded
below. Thus the strict endpoint and the proposed minimum formula are verified
against Boyer's turning equations and classification.

The model is one particle in a fixed external centre/potential, not a closed
relativistic two-body system. The scale \(k/c\) has action units but is supplied
by the coupling and speed ceiling; it does not establish universality or
positive-scale selection.
