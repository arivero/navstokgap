## Findings and qualifications

The source formulas and project transcription agree. Two qualifications must
remain in integration. First, source equation (9) is rigorously an asymptotic
expansion; apply the extremum test to the exact probability curve rather than
to the displayed truncation. Second, the audited inference needs only
\(e^{tX}\) for each Lie-algebra element. It does not rely on the stronger and
generally unavailable statement that every element of an arbitrary connected
matrix Lie group is a single exponential.

The local block calculation and tensor-space intersection are correct. The
seven-dimensional tensor power is only a necessary ambient linear space; the
second-order inequalities and later group arguments impose further
restrictions. Local SO(3) averaging, equations (15) onward, coefficient
elimination, universality, identical-copy closure, and ancilla/discard closure
were outside this audit.

No extraction defect affected the conclusion. Equation (13)'s compact index
layout was checked visually. The archived PDF reports arXiv v1 and its formula
content matches the supplied page renders.
