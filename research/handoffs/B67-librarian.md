# B67 librarian handoff — Q01 first premise audit

## Decision, deliverable and stop

Target: decide whether continuity/purification exclude finite classical
probability for reasons already present in Newtonian continuity or apparatus
ignorance. Deliverable: a two-source premise-to-use map with exact version and
section coverage, classical countermodels, and the remaining action-unit
dependency. Stop reached at the assigned two papers and passage budget; no
full reconstruction proof audit and no prior-art sweep was attempted.

## Result

Hardy quant-ph/0101012v4 makes Axiom 5 an explicit continuous reversible
transformation between any two pure states (§§1, 6--7; HTML `S1.I3`,
`S7/Thmaxiom5`). Axioms 1--4 admit finite classical probability; the finite
simplex has finitely many pure states, so it cannot support the required pure
state path (§4 `S4`). CDP 1011.6451v3 defines five operational principles in
§III.1 and explicitly says classical information theory satisfies all five;
their pure composite states are products. The classical-excluding premise is
the separate purification postulate, with existence and fixed-ancilla
uniqueness under reversible transformations (§III.2 `Thmpostulate1`; first
consequences §IV.2).

## Dependency map

* Hardy ambient class: finite operational state spaces parameterized by
  distinguishability dimension \(N\) and real parameter count \(K\), with
  preparations, measurements, composites and reversible transformations.
* Hardy exclusion: continuity of reversible pure-state transformations.
  Consequence: selects the \(K=N^2\) branch in Hardy's derivation. It is not
  ordinary Newtonian time/position continuity and gives no action scale.
* CDP ambient class: finite operational-probabilistic theories of systems,
  states, tests, effects, transformations and composites.
* CDP exclusion: purification plus essential uniqueness, not apparatus
  ignorance. Consequence: quantum features within their operational package;
  no \(\hbar\), phase rule, or mechanical action lower bound.
* Classical comparison: finite simplex/classical information theory only.
  Do not promote this to a counterexample covering continuous classical phase
  space or Newtonian mechanics.

## Strategic consequence

The Q01 premise cannot be discharged by citing continuity of Newtonian
trajectories or uncertainty about an apparatus. The coordinator should retain
the two reconstruction results as conditional and identify a new physical
selection premise, then separately bridge to a positive dimensional action
constant and its phase/measurement role. The B01 abstract-level coverage is
now upgraded to this bounded exact-passage map; proof status remains
unaudited.
