## Results

- Initial $(X,V)=(0,+u)$, terminal $(0,-u)$, finite $T$, supplied $u<c$ and
  Poisson rate $\lambda$. Conditional odd counts have Bessel-normalized weights;
  independent positive/negative occupation simplexes specify the full path.
- The midpoint atom has mass $1/I_0(\lambda T)$ at $uT/2$. Its exact velocity
  uses the right-continuous path version. A symmetric endpoint window has a
  different limiting midpoint velocity mixture on the one-switch component.
- All cut restrictions come from the same preparation. Fresh quarter-point
  variance on the midpoint atom would violate the speed-saturated half paths.
- Kinetic polygon error is at most $mu^2N_T|\pi|/2$, vanishing almost surely
  and in mean. The return-path action $mu^2T/2$ is a finite-duration cost;
  microscopic duration and speed scaling can drive it to zero.
