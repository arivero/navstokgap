## Original worker findings (qualified by the corrections above)

Balakrishnan, Bena & Van den Broeck, arXiv:cond-mat/0109025v1 / PRE 65 031102, defines the equal-mass hard-point system and thermodynamic limit (pp. 3–6, Eqs. (1)–(8)). For symmetric dichotomic preparation `phi=(delta(V+c)+delta(V-c))/2` (p. 8, Eq. (11)), it gives `C(t)=c^2 exp(-2 n c t)` (p. 8, Eq. (12)). The homogeneous diffusion coefficient is `D=<|U|>/(2n)` (p. 13, Eq. (29)); therefore two-speed `D=c/(2n)=u/(2rho)`. Units are length/time for `c`, inverse length for `n`, and length²/time for `D`.

## Limits and caveats

The construction uses finite free-boundary systems followed by `L,N→∞` at fixed densities; stationarity requires homogeneous left/right preparation. The exact position law contains Bessel/recollision terms (pp. 4–5, 17–18), so the paper’s dichotomic-process discussion is not a blanket pathwise telegraph theorem. No Palm tagged-particle theorem, finite-volume rate, or action plateau is established.
