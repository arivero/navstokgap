## Result

p_a(t)=p(t/a), V_a(Q)=V(Q/a) preserves mass, particle speed, energy values and
stationary pushforward preparation. It multiplies canonical action and A15's
covariance estimator by a. Force and acceleration scale by 1/a. A class
admitting arbitrarily small such contractions of one positive finite observable
has zero positive-value infimum. The conclusion is conditional on model-class
closure; fixed Kepler coupling and uniform force bounds can obstruct this map.
