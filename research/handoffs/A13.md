## Result and limits

Two independent uniformly shifted lattices, each spacing $a=2/\rho$ and
speed $\pm u$, have the same mass, density, one-particle velocity variance,
energy density and mean collision rate as A12's gas. They change joint
position–velocity correlations, including independent marking. Their invariant
phase law is not mixing. A typical tag has uniform sign and uniform first
residual wait on $(0,d)$, followed by constant waits $d=1/(\rho u)$.
Its velocity is a stationary square wave, and displacement is periodic.

For $s$ the duration folded into $[0,d]$ modulo $2d$, displacement variance
is $u^2(s^2-2s^3/(3d))$. Thus $\mathsf h(\Delta)\le
m/(3\rho^2\Delta)\to0$. Infinite gas is fixed first; total energy is
infinite but energy density finite. Speeds remain bounded and binary
collisions conserve momentum and energy. Absolute tag position starts at zero.
