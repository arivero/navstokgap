## Result and proof

For regular closed $C^2$ trajectories in Euclidean dimension $d\ge2$, let
$v\ge v_*>0$, $p=P(v)n$ and $|\dot p|\le F_{\max}$ in one frame.
Fenchel–Borsuk gives $\mathcal K=\int|\dot n|dt\ge2\pi$; orthogonality gives
$|\dot p|^2=\dot P^2+P^2|\dot n|^2$. Hence
$T\ge P_*\mathcal K/F_{\max}$ and
$J\ge P_*^2v_*\mathcal K/(2\pi F_{\max})\ge P_*^2v_*/F_{\max}$.
A circle in a smooth cutoff central potential attains equality, proving
sharpness over the admissible model class. This is normalized canonical
orbit action; general covariance identities and vector potentials are separate.
