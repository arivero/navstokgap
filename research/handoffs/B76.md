## Finding and source-to-model lesson

Bacciagaluppi §2.3, p. 4 is an exact structural precedent for the local
many-to-one preparation quotient. Cavalcanti et al. pp. 2--4 are a close
conceptual precedent for transformations splitting preparation equivalence,
but use nonlinear quantum boxes and no-signalling rather than a classical
Hamiltonian quotient. Neither source matches the explicit spin pair or finite
Koopman/expectation obstruction.

The direct lesson is terminological and structural: operational equivalence
must be indexed to the admitted transformations and measurements. P06's pair
is equivalent for the initial retained terminal tests, then ceases to be so
when the Hamiltonian-precomposed tests are admitted. The paper should describe
failure of dynamical stability of the old quotient rather than unqualified
equivalence inside the enlarged theory.
