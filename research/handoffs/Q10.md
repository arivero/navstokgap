## Result and consequence

The [derivation](../../notes/thermal-receiver-reliability.md) gives exact
necessary and sufficient feasibility conditions and the optimized barrier
reduction k_B T [log(c tau/(a t_g))]_+, provided nu t_g>=c. Here
a=-log(1-epsilon) and c=log(1/eta). It includes the zero-bound and infeasible
branches. A shared prefactor cancels from the contrast, but changing it during
the gate defeats an inference to barrier reduction from probabilities alone.
An incident-work bound additionally needs u<=chi W; reset/control work is not
identified with a barrier height. The result constrains a declared receiver
class, not a universal action constant.
