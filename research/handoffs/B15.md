## Verified metadata and findings

1. **Feynman–Hibbs:** *Quantum Mechanics and Path Integrals*, McGraw–Hill,
   New York, 1965, ISBN 0-07-020650-3. Problem 2-6 is reported at first
   edition pp. 34–36. Status: `metadata` plus location lead, not passage.
2. **Gaveau et al.:** B. Gaveau, T. Jacobson, M. Kac, L. S. Schulman,
   “Relativistic Extension of the Analogy between Quantum Mechanics and Brownian
   Motion,” *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 53, 419–422 (1984), DOI
   10.1103/PhysRevLett.53.419. APS abstract read. It distinguishes a real
   Poisson process/telegrapher equation from analytic continuation/Dirac.
3. **Jacobson–Schulman:** Theodore Jacobson and L. S. Schulman, “Quantum
   stochastics: the passage from a relativistic to a non-relativistic path
   integral,” *J. Phys. A* 17, 375–383 (1984), DOI 10.1088/0305-4470/17/2/023.
   Metadata and indexed abstract/formula lead read. It contrasts relativistic
   \(\Delta x\sim\Delta t\) with Brownian \((\Delta x)^2\sim\Delta t\) and
   names the Compton scale \(\hbar/(mc)\).

## Critical review and unresolved points

The positive Poisson reversal rate is a stochastic parameter; the checkerboard
corner weight is complex and belongs to an amplitude sum. Analytic continuation
does not turn a probability rate into a universal positive action scale. The
1984 records do not, at this reading depth, establish COM mass-additivity of a
diffusion/action coefficient, identify any such coefficient with \(\hbar\), or
prove a lower action gap. Exact recurrence formulas, normalization, limits,
and Klein–Gordon versus Dirac component conventions remain unverified.
