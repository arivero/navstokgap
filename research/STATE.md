## Goals and current proof boundaries

physical $\hbar$ for Newtonian completion, and construction of pure $SU(3)$
Yang–Mills on $\mathbb R^4$ with a finite positive physical spectral gap.
Classical existence, conditional action floors, quantum states/dynamics/
instruments and universality or calibration of an action scale remain
separate claims. For a stated $S_0$, $\epsilon=\hbar/S_0$ differs from
mesh, lattice spacing and record resolution. QCD's pion remains a
[symmetry benchmark](../notes/three-continuum-limits.md).
