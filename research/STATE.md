The first priority is an explanation of positive physical action from the
central-force trajectory limit itself. Newton connects [Lemma X's force
measurement](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#lemma-x-and-the-measurement-of-initial-force)
to the orbital force rule in Book I, Proposition VI (1713).
For a [Kepler pair selected by binding energy](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#a-radius-count-beyond-the-kepler-turning-point),
can a calibrated detector count pairs beyond the outer turning radius?
The [bare lithium fraction from helium-6](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#a-stronger-coulomb-attraction-and-the-measured-bare-lithium-fraction)
tests the work an outgoing beta can supply to a bound electron.
For [neutrons returning under gravity](../notes/dirichlet-wall-impulse.md#counting-turning-heights-from-neutron-positions),
does a height measurement at an independently randomized time assign a
negative count to any interval of turning heights?
[Polonium-212 escape](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#a-clock-on-escape-through-a-central-force-barrier)
tests whether the radial work measured on an incident alpha also bounds
every outgoing channel. An absolute exterior count on the decay clock
would then bound the action.
[Four central-force releases](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#four-central-force-releases-measured-by-final-speeds)
test arbitrary launch distributions using only the final kinetic energies.
Do their variances and third moments violate the radial trajectory bound
with unrecorded switch work bounded and complete counts? A fifth
Coulomb strength measures the force factor from the same flights.
[Hydrogen fragment spectra](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#what-two-hydrogen-fragment-spectra-already-test)
give a fourfold ratio excess under common capture. The relative capture
response and rotational spread remain open.
[Single-atom lattice returns](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#a-single-atom-returning-in-a-tilted-lattice)
need axial work, loss and freeze calibrations. [Two passages through one barrier](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#two-images-of-a-returning-orbit)
bound the barrier-top exception with four launch energies.
The potassium [two-power test](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#locating-a-turn-with-two-barrier-powers)
uses guarded returns and a common optical work calibration.
For a harmonic central force the [trapped-ion contrast estimates](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#three-measured-contrasts-of-a-trapped-ion)
exceed the additive phase ceiling by $0.268$ at one preparation. Can
the probe's rms displacement error be bounded below $0.268/(2\pi)$
of its fringe pitch after other errors? For [atom photographs](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#two-action-scales-in-the-same-atom-photographs),
does the bounded average of raw positions fall below zero with the
hold times, camera response and missing launches accounted for?
For a freely rotating pair a separate [three-angle test](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#three-angle-readings-on-a-central-orbit)
allows for radial motion.
The [point-insertion test](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#central-force-passage-under-point-insertion)
exposes the sensitivity of one orbit to its preparation. The
[three-energy scattering test](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#a-three-energy-test-of-central-force-trajectories)
cancels any common impact distribution. Internal states can fit the
[three helium-3 gas pressures](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#a-classical-atom-that-reproduces-the-three-coefficients).
[Liquid calorimetry](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#how-many-atomic-motions-can-the-measured-heat-support)
excludes freely mobile classical Gibbs atoms even with internal
states. [Isotope enrichment](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#isotope-exchange-as-a-test-of-central-forces)
also excludes changing inertial mass alone for constrained atoms.
The liquid test still needs a bound on isotope exchange work including
forces that first appear in the presence of a third atom.
For individual trajectories the [argon transmission and finite work
tests](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#an-exterior-force-test-with-argon)
require an independent exterior force calibration. The [77 K shutter
and field-free tests](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#a-thermal-test-with-two-shutters)
need the binding work and its slope during passage outside $0.40$ nm
for a drift velocity and a fixed-time position measurement, respectively.
Earlier apparatus tests use [three positions and two ions](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md),
an [electron and shutter](../notes/galileo-two-path-interference.md#two-slits-and-a-position-detector)
and a [clocked grating](../notes/groupoid-quantum-newton-states.md#9-action-calibration-with-a-clocked-grating).

## Goals and current proof boundaries

The two open goals are independently justified necessity of a positive
physical $\hbar$ for Newtonian completion, and construction of pure $SU(3)$
Yang–Mills on $\mathbb R^4$ with a finite positive physical spectral gap.
Classical existence, conditional action floors, quantum states/dynamics/
instruments and universality or calibration of an action scale remain
separate claims. For a stated $S_0$, $\epsilon=\hbar/S_0$ differs from
mesh, lattice spacing and record resolution. QCD's pion remains a
[symmetry benchmark](../notes/three-continuum-limits.md).

For Newton, the [classical trajectory construction](../notes/balaban-ode-lesson.md)
remains available. [Quantum lifting for Lipschitz forces and finite spin
networks](../notes/lipschitz-certificate-quantum-lift.md) takes the action
as an input. The missing physical premise
must survive the [cusp and history tests](../notes/newton-cusp-preparation-floor.md),
[selective meter counterexample](../notes/gaussian-meter-entropy-floor.md)
and [complete-record controller models](../notes/sed-closure-under-recording.md).
Coherent point-record behavior obeys the stated
[frequency and preparation conditions](../notes/zero-branch-reachability.md).
The [scalar scaling map](../notes/groupoid-quantum-newton-states.md#7-interacting-kernels-under-tangent-scaling)
retains the complete kernels and normalized endpoint bridges.

For gauge theory, retained-barrier weak-strip (112)/(116) and quadratic
source test (177) leave nonlinear (113), physical recoupling, $SU(3)$
comparison and iteration open
([argument](../notes/su2-midplane-small-field.md)).
[Generated-action matching](../notes/four-dimensional-composition.md), the
[small-volume bridge](../notes/weak-coupling-feshbach-reduction.md),
continuum reconstruction and [spectral weight across all physical
sectors](../notes/mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md#what-a-tensor-pole-determines)
retain separate obligations. The [valley correction](../notes/torus-valley-potential.md#4-saturation-and-central-holonomies)
removes the unsupported global perturbation inference. The committee's
[10 November assessment of the October pure-gauge submission](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/YM)
accepts a Wightman scaling limit and leaves infinite-volume uniqueness
and the physical mass gap open. On 18 November the group still lists
the 4 and 15 November submissions as under assessment.
