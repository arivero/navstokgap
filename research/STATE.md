## Newton's central-force trajectory limit

Does the central-force argument in the *Principia* require a physical
principle that excludes zero action under arbitrarily fine subdivision?
A physical premise specifies which motions or continuations the dynamics
admit. It needs a justification independent of $\hbar$.

In Lemma X Newton changed “regular” force in 1687 to finite force in
1713. The new Corollary 4 relates force to the initial displacement
divided by squared time. Proposition VI then invokes it for orbital
motion. The immediate question is where that argument requires a limit
for each given trajectory and whether any step requires a bound uniform
over a family of trajectories. Specify the force law, the class of
orbits and what is held fixed as points are inserted.
[Edition comparison](../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#lemma-x-and-the-measurement-of-initial-force).

Two existing results constrain any answer. A globally Lipschitz force
admits the same classical motion on every refining sequence of positive
time partitions. At a continuous finite central force with a cusp, two
continuations from the same incoming orbit can have the same initial
force coefficient and cell actions on every subdivision through the
joining time. Determine which dynamics, if any, require an additional
principle there and whether it rules out every classical continuation.
[Polygon theorem](../notes/balaban-ode-lesson.md) and
[fixed-force counterexample](../notes/balaban-newton-time-blocking.md#central-force-passage-under-point-insertion).

A vanishing action along a particular orbit or time cell differs from a
vanishing physical action parameter. A quantum lift exists for every
supplied positive action in its stated class. A separate foundational
note is due when an independently justified premise actually excludes
zero physical action. Universality and the value of $\hbar$ remain open.
[Action scales](../notes/action-unit-dimensional-selection.md) and
[quantum lift](../notes/lipschitz-certificate-quantum-lift.md).

## Four-dimensional Yang–Mills

The goal is continuum pure $SU(3)$ on $\mathbb R^4$ with a finite positive
physical spectral gap. The [pion](../notes/three-continuum-limits.md) is
a symmetry benchmark.

For gauge theory, retained-barrier weak-strip (112)/(116) and quadratic
source test (177) leave nonlinear (113), physical recoupling, $SU(3)$
comparison and iteration open
([argument](../notes/su2-midplane-small-field.md)).
[Generated-action matching](../notes/four-dimensional-composition.md), the
[small-volume bridge](../notes/weak-coupling-feshbach-reduction.md),
continuum reconstruction and [spectral weight across all physical
sectors](../notes/mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md#what-a-tensor-pole-determines)
retain separate obligations. The [valley correction](../notes/torus-valley-potential.md#4-saturation-and-central-holonomies)
removes the unsupported global perturbation inference. The committee's
[10 November assessment of the October pure-gauge submission](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/YM)
accepts a Wightman scaling limit and leaves infinite-volume uniqueness
and the physical mass gap open. On 18 November the group still lists
the 4 and 15 November submissions as under assessment.
