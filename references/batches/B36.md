The model uses a joint Hamiltonian `H+H_app+H_int`, with impulsive
`H_int=alpha delta(t-t0) A Q`, pointer `P` conjugate to probe `Q`, and a trap
released at measurement. The apparatus is thermalized at `T=1/(beta k_B)` in
a harmonic trapped mode of angular frequency `Omega`, then isolated. Its ready
state in the source's unit-mass canonical normal form is Gaussian with
variances `Var(Q)=1/(beta Omega^2)` and `Var(P)=1/beta` (11).
Coordinator corrected both variances from the initial worker transcription.
The pointer record is stable after release because `P` is
in involution with the apparatus' own Hamiltonian; the record is read as
`A*=-P*/alpha`.

The paper defines measurement strength `k` with dimensions `[A]^-2` and
inverse quality `qbar=2/(beta Omega)=2 k_B T/Omega` with dimensions of action. Its exact relation is
`epsilon_A eta_A = qbar/2`, where `epsilon_A=(alpha^2 beta)^(-1/2)` and
`eta_A=(2 k qbar^2)^(1/2)`; eta measures spread of the flow parameter generated
by A, with units action/[A], rather than the rms change of an arbitrary second
observable. Thus the positive scale is apparatus quality, not a
universal constant. The text explicitly says it can be reduced by cooling or
tightening the trap and associates the unattainable zero limit with the third
law. No uniform positive lower bound follows: temperature, trap curvature,
mode frequency and apparatus resources are allowed to vary, and the coupling
is impulsive. The source does not supply a finite-duration pulse bound, fixed
total apparatus mass, or a clock model beyond the interaction time parameter.

Established source result: within this specified model, finite-temperature
thermal preparation produces a precision–disturbance product and stable pointer
record. Project inference: it cannot by itself establish R06's positive action
floor under a fixed-resource finite-duration class. Action positivity is strict
for each finite-quality apparatus, while the infimum over permitted preparations
is zero unless an additional resource constraint is imposed.

Actionable R06 suggestion: replace the delta pulse by a bounded coupling over
duration `Delta`, include a clock degree of freedom and a record pointer, then
state fixed total mass, force/coupling bounds, and a lower preparation-energy or
temperature constraint. Derive whether those constraints imply a nonzero lower
bound on `qbar` (rather than assuming that thermal preparation does so).
