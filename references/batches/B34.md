# B34: three-body receiver memory and finite-history recovery

## Result

The R04 draft is algebraically consistent. With the centre mode removed, p1=(2/3)P, mu=3m/2, nu=m/2; eliminating (y,Q) gives exact cosine memory and two inherited initial quadratures. Tagged phase alone does not compose, while carrying the receiver pair does.

## Written audit

The centre constraint yields restricted kinetic energy 3m xdot^2/4+m ydot^2/4, hence the stated reduced masses. a=9k/4,g=3k/4,d=5k/4 and ad-g^2=9k^2/4>0, proving positive quadratic energy and global smooth flow. Omega^2=d/nu=5k/(2m); integration by parts gives a_eff=9k/5 and Gamma=(9k/20) cos(Omega t), with force-dimensional convolution and slip. At a cut, inherited (y_tau,Q_tau), not a fresh conditional draw, is exact.

The block identity A_(s+t)=A_t A_s+B_t C_s identifies the lost excursion. Fixed-energy states x=P=Q=0, y=+/-sqrt(2E/d) share tagged phase but have opposite acceleration. The displayed small-delta block gives det B_delta=g^2 delta^4/(12 mu nu)+O(delta^6)>0, so sufficiently close two-time phase observations recover the hidden pair. This is a derived finite-dimensional result; inverse blow-up is conditioning, not an action floor.

Normal frequencies sqrt(k/m),sqrt(3k/m) give the stated modal-action bounds at fixed positive energy. Scaling coordinates/momenta by b scales actions and energy by b^2 without removing memory, so preparation-independent positivity does not follow.
