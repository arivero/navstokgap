# B08 result map: regulator limits

The bounded audit found established ingredients for Gaussian bridges, oscillatory weak limits, and exact quadratic stationary phase. The two simultaneous-regulator constructions are proved correctly in the draft but were not found as exact literature statements in this coverage. This is a coverage classification, not a novelty endorsement.

## Coverage and stopping point

Four primary sources and 13 selected printed pages were used: Pitman--Yor pp. 6, 10, 12--13; Rivero pp. 1--3; Rindler pp. 16, 56--57; and Guillemin--Sternberg pp. 463--465. All imported formulas were checked visually. Wilson--Kogut was excluded as assigned to the coordinator.

One targeted query family was used: exact quadratic stationary phase with Fourier normalization, determinant, signature, and author/institution-hosted notes. The supplied Zworski 404 route was not retried. The search surfaced MIT/Stanford/Berkeley author pages; Guillemin--Sternberg was selected because equation (14.7) is an exact quadratic identity under a stated Fourier convention. Search stopped after filling the fourth-source slot.

## Source-to-model test

The next test should discriminate topology and dynamics rather than repeat normalization. Impose one of the following on the sine/bridge sequences and calculate whether a positive action defect survives:

1. uniform acceleration/force control, which excludes the displayed sine sequence because (\|\ddot q_n\|_\infty=2\pi n v_*/T);
2. strong (L^2) velocity convergence, which forces the quadratic kinetic defect to zero;
3. a finite-speed relativistic action with a fixed strict speed margin, testing whether bounded speed alone still permits a nonzero defect.

For the stochastic branch, state a path-space topology and a force/interacting drift class, then ask whether the common-noise coupling and uniform convergence survive while a quadratic energy remains noncontinuous. This directly tests which physical premise could select the surviving (d\kappa) scale.
