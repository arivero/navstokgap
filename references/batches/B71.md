# B71: detailed-balance Hermitian transfer

The square-root stationary-density transform used in C126 is the
standard stochastic-matrix-form/Rokhsar--Kivelson construction. The selected
literature supports Hermiticity, the square-root Gibbs ground state, equality
of relaxation and excitation spectra, locality inherited from local moves, and
the freedom in the stochastic rates. It does not identify the resulting
operator with independently given physical real-time dynamics or supply an
action constant. G04 should therefore retain the construction as a conditional
stochastic parent Hamiltonian.

[Source companion](../../docs/batches/B71/ising-hermitian-source-companion.md),
[proof/source review](../../reviews/ising-hermitian-B71.md).

- C. L. Henley, DOI `10.1088/0953-8984/16/11/045`, arXiv
  `cond-mat/0311345v1`, PDF p. 7, Eq. (3.7): detailed balance symmetrizes the
  transition matrix by square-root equilibrium weights; the spectrum is
  unchanged and local classical moves give local quantum hopping terms.
- C. Castelnovo, C. Chamon, C. Mudry and P. Pujol, DOI
  `10.1016/j.aop.2005.01.006`, arXiv `cond-mat/0502068v1`, PDF pp. 8--10 and
  12--15, Eqs. (8)--(15), (19)--(28): positive-semidefinite local blocks have a
  square-root Boltzmann zero mode; detailed balance gives the master equation;
  relaxation eigenvalues equal the negatives of Hamiltonian eigenvalues; and
  the symmetric move coefficients remain free, with Glauber dynamics included
  as a choice.

Literature status: the general mapping and its spectral/locality properties are
established source results. The exact three-site heat-bath operator and gap in
C126 are project specializations using C124; no exact prior publication of that
formula was established. No novelty claim is made.

Source-to-model suggestion: treat the rate parameter as part of the parent
Hamiltonian's coupling data, not as fixed by the Gibbs state. A later physical
identification must independently derive the clock-to-time conversion and the
positive action scale before C126's inverse-time gap becomes an energy gap.
