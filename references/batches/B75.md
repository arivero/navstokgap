# B75: publication prior art for the elastic-gas comparison

The complete C050--C051 construction was unmatched in a four-query, two-source, eight-page audit. The closest source, Leibovich and Barkai, already makes the teaching comparison that random and equally spaced preparations at fixed density produce different tagged transport under common hard-core Brownian dynamics. Cividini and Kundu supplies broader arbitrary-initial-condition context. Initial-correlation dependence therefore cannot be presented as the draft's new result.

The specific Hamiltonian comparison remains unmatched within this bounded coverage: marked Poisson positions versus two independently shifted opposite-sign lattices at common $m,u,\rho$, energy density and mean tagged collision rate $\rho u$, yielding respectively $D=u/(2\rho)$ and exact periodic cancellation with $D=0$. This is a bounded literature result, not a novelty or priority claim.
