C124's model and slow magnetization mode match Glauber's 1963 kinetic Ising
chain, while its all-mode lower bound is a project derivation using a standard
Hamming coupling. The bounded audit found no contradiction and makes no
novelty claim. See the [source companion](../../docs/batches/B68/ising-gap-source-companion.md)
and [proof/source review](../../reviews/ising-gap-B68.md).

  Eqs. (9), (17), (30), and (44)--(51) match the rate convention,
  `gamma=tanh(2b)`, finite-ring one-spin modes, and magnetization decay
  `a(1-gamma)`. Reading used a public third-party indexed scan/transcription;

  maximal coupling and adjacent-to-global contraction. Its theorem is
  discrete-time mixing theory, not C124's continuous-time full spectral-gap
  equality.
