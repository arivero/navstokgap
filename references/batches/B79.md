# B79: floor-to-gap in Yang--Mills quantum mechanics

Source batch for [G08](../../notes/action-floor-yang-mills-gap.md). It reuses
[B78](B78.md) for the model and adds two entries.

- B. Simon, Ann. Phys. 146 (1983) 209, p. 3 (Caltech reprint 158,
  fetched and read at passage level): the classical phase-space volume of
  $-\Delta+x^2y^2$ is infinite for every energy, "the true behavior" of the
  counting function is $N(E)\sim E^{3/2}\ln E$ (his ref. [7], not read), and
  the Cwikel--Lieb--Rosenbljum bound validates half of the classical
  intuition. Used for Remark 3 of the note.
- L. D. Landau and E. M. Lifshitz, Mechanics, 3rd ed., §49 "Adiabatic
  invariants": metadata from general knowledge, section number unverified
  this session; the note uses only the elementary ellipse-area identity,
  proved in Proposition 1.

Coverage limits: no search for prior "old quantum theory" treatments of the
$x^2y^2$ or matrix-model spectrum was run; the Bohr--Sommerfeld levels are
labelled semiclassical estimates and no novelty is asserted.
