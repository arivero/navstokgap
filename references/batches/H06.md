## Findings that touch the programme

- Archimedes' four-thirds rule reproduces C001's lens area exactly: the
  inscribed triangle on the chord with apex at the half-time point has area
  $Fv\tau^3/(16m)$, and four-thirds of it is $Fv\tau^3/(12m)$; checked symbolically.
- Euler's first application of $\int Mv\,ds$ minimum is uniform gravity,
  $v^2=a+gx$, the constant-force setting of C001.
- Hamilton's characteristic function is the fixed-energy action; the
  fixed-duration $\Delta S$ of C001 is a difference of his 1835 principal function.
- Jacobi's proposed name is “Princip des kleinsten Kraftaufwandes” (archive
  line 576), and lecture 6 opens by calling “kleinste Wirkung” a false name
  and warning against overweighting the existence of a minimum.
- Gregory's preface prints the claim that the ancients knew mutual gravitation
  and its law, the content Newton drafted in the Classical Scholia; this is a
  pre-1701 printed witness for H02/H03.
- Plutarch's section 39 now has searchable English and Greek beside the 1570
  Latin pages of H04.

- English Wikisource lacks the Fourth Day of Two New Sciences.
- Kac 1974 and Leibniz 1686 were located earlier in the session and left for a
  later batch; Kac is open access at Project Euclid.
