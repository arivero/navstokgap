## Result

The three B12 draft results are elementary consequences of their stated
finite-dimensional models. The source audit supports the Gaussian bridge and
Markov restriction background, but supplies no physical selection principle
for a positive action scale. No novelty or complete Newton-history claim is
made.

## Source coverage and stopping point

The one source read for this bounded audit was Pitman–Yor (2018), arXiv
1802.09679v1, selected printed pp. 6, 10, 12–13 (six pages maximum): eqs.
(4)–(5), §3.4, and eqs. (20)–(25). The existing Newton companion was used as a
source route for the already audited historical projectile passages (printed
pp. 89, 99–101); no new Newton source search was undertaken. Search stopped
because these routes match the background hypotheses while leaving the
physical selection question open.

The source does not support uniform path-space convergence, an intervention
law at a cut, or a universal positive \(\kappa\). The finite-dimensional
restriction argument also presumes the same physical times, endpoints and
mass in the coarse and fine laws, and \(\kappa\ge0\); the endpoint-only
partition has no interior marginal and therefore imposes no constraint.

## Source-driven next test

Specify a physically executed cut, rather than coordinate insertion, and test
whether its law preserves energy/momentum while producing the conditional
variance above. The first bounded implementation should compare the Gaussian
prediction \(\kappa/2\) with a candidate finite-speed intervention; only then
test scale universality and the classical limit.
