Continuity of inversion then preserves nonzero columns for sufficiently small fixed \tau. The remaining transfer from narrow smooth pulses to the ideal rows is a model-specific limiting argument: it must state pulse supports, normalization, and a uniform operator-norm remainder after row normalization. It cannot be inferred from Hermite interpolation alone.
