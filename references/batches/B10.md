# B10: elastic collision bath and action relaxation

Result: the elastic affine update and constant-rate Poisson collision process
are established prior model ingredients. The stationary covariance plateau,
physical-time variance relaxation, and bath-rescaling countermodel are not
found as exact theorem statements in the selected literature; for the stated
refreshed iid model they follow by short generator/conditioning calculations.

## Ranked source matches

### 1. Barkai (2003), exact model match for C022 premises

Eli Barkai, “Stable Equilibrium Based on Levy Statistics: A Linear Boltzmann
Equation Approach,” arXiv:cond-mat/0303255v1 (13 March 2003),
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0303255.

Selected pp. 1, 3–5, including coordinator review. Equations (2)–(5) give the elastic affine map and master
equation; (8)–(12) give the Poisson collision-count mixture. The source assumes
uniform rate, molecular chaos, and a bath PDF unchanged by collisions. It does
not impose bounded bath velocities or B10's `m >= M`; those are additional
assumptions used for a compact invariant interval and nonnegative coefficient.

### 2. Barbier and Trizac (2012), exact lag-covariance specialization

Matthieu Barbier and Emmanuel Trizac, “Field Induced Stationary State for an
Accelerated Tracer in a Bath,” J. Stat. Phys. 149 (2012),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6759, DOI:10.1007/s10955-012-0591-x.

Selected pp. 1, 19. Appendix (89)–(98) gives stationary velocity covariance
and its integrated diffusion coefficient; at Maxwell kernel exponent `nu=0`,
(95)–(98) give exponential lag decay and the integral `Gamma(0)/(1-a)`.
Restoring a constant collision rate gives the stationary `2m S*/gamma` value.
The source's field-free specialization supports the lag observable, not the
nonstationary preparation-time ODE.

### 3. Ben-Naim and Krapivsky (2003), contrast for clock dependence

E. Ben-Naim and P. L. Krapivsky, “The Inelastic Maxwell Model,”
arXiv:cond-mat/0301238v1 (14 January 2003),
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0301238.

Selected pp. 1, 6. The review gives exponential autocorrelation in collision
number but a cooling, time-dependent collision rate and hence aging/algebraic
physical-time behavior. This is a bounded contrast, not an exact match: B10's
clock is independent Poisson with fixed `nu`, and its bath is refreshed iid.

## Coverage limits and next test

No source in the bounded reading derives a spatial encounter law for the
refreshed model. The next source-driven test is therefore to replace `nu` by a
one-dimensional encounter rate from a bath density and relative-speed flux,
then determine whether the resulting rate scales as `(m+M)s^2/H*` or leaves an
independent density/geometry factor. This directly tests whether a spatial
realization can remove the C024 rescaling freedom; it must keep the incoming
velocity bias and distinguish the closed-gas detailed-balance problem from the
refreshed reservoir.
