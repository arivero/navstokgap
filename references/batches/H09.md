# H09: verification of the 1634 letter, Aristotle's division argument, Guldin, and the Latin reception of the atom

Date: 2026-09-08. One Claude Fable 5.1 session, no subagents. The user asked for
all five follow-ups of H08.

## Results

1. **Scan verification.** Printed pp. 135–138 of Opere XVI were rendered from the
   archive PDF and read. The bowl-and-cone reply is letter no. 2992, Cavalieri
   to Galileo in Arcetri, Bologna, 2 October 1634, Mss. Gal. P. VI, T. XII,
   car. 85–86, autograph. H08 had dated it 12 September because a generic
   header pattern missed the bracketed header; the excerpt, companion and batch
   note are corrected, and the anchors now cite printed page and line.
2. **De generatione I.2.** Greek text from Greek Wikisource and the 1866
   French of Barthélemy-Saint-Hilaire from French Wikisource, both with the
   division-everywhere argument anchored.
3. **Guldin.** The Max Planck Institute for the History of Science serves the
   complete Centrobaryca with a IIIF API. Book IV's preface (canvases
   0397–0403) and the opening of chapter V against Cavalieri (printed
   pp. 339–345, canvases 0735–0741) are stored as page images. The preface
   states that Galileo's Dialogue on local motion concludes against Cavalieri;
   pages 3–4 are transcribed from the images.
4. **Latin reception of the kalām atom.** Aquinas, Summa contra Gentiles
   III.69, on the Loquentes in lege Maurorum, from the Corpus Thomisticum;
   Duns Scotus, Opus Oxoniense II d.2 in the Vivès edition, with the arguments
   that time is not composed of indivisibles and that a traversed space is not
   composed of points. Averroes' Destructio destructionum exists only in
   sixteenth-century Latin scans and was left out.
5. **Test 1 of I003.** [The two-faces note](../../notes/cut-paradox-two-faces.md)
   proves that a scalar, translation-invariant unitary evolution with
   light-cone support is a uniform translation with a constant phase, the
   Lorentzian counterpart of C032, and places the obstruction of remark 2 at the
   Compton wave number; nine checks run in `make check`.

## Access limits

The Opere PDF has no text layer, so pages were located through the archive's
full-text index and read as images. Scotus's concentric-circles argument was
not found by keyword in the Vivès OCR. Guldin's chapter V beyond p. 345 was
not stored, and no OCR exists for the record. The Corpus Thomisticum text is
an edited scholarly edition served for study.
