## Exact source matches and derived consequences

* Cinque exact match (passage): conditioning on initial velocity and switch count gives an affine alternating sum (Remark 2.1, (2.4)); Theorem 2.1, (2.6), gives the conditional density of the odd alternating sum. The equal-rate specialization used by C033/C037 follows by setting the rate difference to zero.
* Derived in notes/bridge-crossover.md: splitting the C033 return bridge at the midpoint, summing the two midpoint-velocity sectors, proving the combinatorial factor S_k, and obtaining the beta density. Cinque does not state this conditioned return-bridge midpoint law.
* Endpoint atom/version: the k=0 one-switch bridge has its switch at T/2, hence a deterministic midpoint atom of mass 1/I0(z). This is a consequence of the C033 bridge conditioning and its right-continuous velocity protocol, not a source theorem.
* Bogachev–Ratanov exact scope: the opened abstract/source concerns occupation time of a spatial half-line and its long-time arcsine limit. It does not condition on the return endpoint or supply the C037 midpoint law; no formula was imported.
* DLMF exact match: 10.32.1 supplies the integral representation used to justify concentration of the normalized exponential weight. The I0'/I0 limit, l’Hôpital calculation, and g(z) limit are the note’s derivation.

## Proof and scope audit

The factorial identity and Jacobian are algebraically consistent with the count-conditioned telegraph densities; the interior density integrates to one for every k>=1, while k=0 is handled separately as an atom. Beta integration gives equal first and second conditional moments 1/(2k+1), and the count mixture gives R(z). The cubic expansion and unscaled mean limit are correct. The latter retains the boundary bias E[Y] -> u/(2 lambda), while E[Q] -> 0.

No proof defect was found in the stated domain m,u,lambda,T>0, 0<u<c. The endpoint atom must remain explicit; silently treating all counts as an interior density would be wrong. The large-window argument uses l’Hôpital and the DLMF integral representation, not monotonicity. No monotonicity claim for g is supported or needed.

The necessary bound K* <= m u^2 T (and sharper midpoint bound kappa_mid <= m u^2 T/4) is not a sufficiency statement. If masses approach zero while speed and a finite observation window are common, a common nonnegative coefficient forces K*=0. Positive universal plateaux thus require mass-dependent crossover windows; this is a derived consequence, not prior art. Identifying H* with hbar or u with c remains a separate model hypothesis.
