## Findings

The scalar central-fibre radius sum is an R12 derivation from Cartesian fibres,
central symmetry, and sign-aligned extremizers. The centre/relative formulas and
identical-copy `H=nH_1` are derived consequences of that law and deterministic
aligned errors, not A08 variance composition. The aggregate-record image,
single-body reduction, and strict Cauchy–Schwarz loss are also derived under
freely split forces/errors; neither source directly matches them. The mass-only
closure follows from nonnegative additivity and rational squeezing, with the
positive extensive product conditional on the stated closure premises. A
mass-independent positive product is excluded by identical-copy composition.
