# B78: low-dimensional mass gaps and Yang--Mills quantum mechanics

Source batch for [G07](../../notes/low-dimensional-mass-gap.md). Each entry
records what the source establishes and the reading level attained in this
session (metadata, abstract, passage). The batch supports written proofs of
established results; it asserts no novelty and no exhaustive coverage.
Metadata for the abstract- and metadata-level entries were verified through
INSPIRE and Crossref records; BibTeX is in `references/library.bib`.

## Passage level

- B. Simon, "Some quantum operators with discrete spectrum but classically
  continuous spectrum", Ann. Phys. 146 (1983) 209--220
  ([Caltech reprint 158](https://math.caltech.edu/SimonPapers/158.pdf)).
  Sections 1, 2, 5 and 7 read: the $x^2y^2$ operator has purely discrete
  spectrum; the first proof is the zero-point bound
  $H\ge\tfrac12(-\Delta+|x|+|y|)$, display (5); the "sliced bread"
  inequalities are the fourth proof (Section 5, Theorem 1); Corollary 4
  gives discrete spectrum for the semisimple Lie-algebra model with at least
  two matrices. Used for Theorems 5--6 of the note.
- B. Simon, "Schrödinger operators with purely discrete spectrum", Methods
  Funct. Anal. Topology 15 (2009) 61--66
  ([Caltech reprint 322](https://math.caltech.edu/SimonPapers/322.pdf)):
  corroborates Reed--Simon IV Sect. XIII.1 and Theorem XIII.16.
- B. Simon, "Tosio Kato's work on non-relativistic quantum mechanics",
  arXiv:1711.00528 (2017), Section 7: Kato's self-adjointness theorem for
  $V\ge0$, $V\in L^2_{\rm loc}$, and the radial Hardy inequality (7.26).
- A. Jaffe and E. Witten, Quantum Yang--Mills theory,
  [local PDF](../../docs/JaffeWitten_YangMills.pdf) pp. 6--7 and 12: the
  uniform-in-volume gap remarks quoted in the note.

## Abstract level

- M. Lüscher, Nucl. Phys. B219 (1983) 233; M. Lüscher and G. Münster,
  Nucl. Phys. B232 (1984) 445; B. de Wit, M. Lüscher and H. Nicolai,
  Nucl. Phys. B320 (1989) 135 (INSPIRE): discrete torus spectrum and
  $g^{2/3}$ expansion; one-loop SU(2) levels and crossover near
  $z=M(0^+)L\simeq2$; continuous spectrum of supersymmetric matrix models.
- K. Osterwalder and E. Seiler, Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440: positivity,
  strong-coupling infinite-volume limit, Wilson confinement bound; the
  strong-coupling mass gap is the standard cluster-expansion consequence,
  not read at section level.
- A. M. Polyakov, Nucl. Phys. B120 (1977) 429: monopole mechanism for the
  three-dimensional compact $U(1)$ photon mass.
- M. Göpfert and G. Mack, Commun. Math. Phys. 82 (1982) 545: mass gap and
  confinement of the 3D Villain model for all couplings, Debye mass
  $m_D^2=(2\beta/a^3)e^{-\beta v(0)/2}$, $\beta=4\pi^2/e^2$ (transcribed
  from the abstract record; placement of constants may carry ambiguity).
- A. H. Guth, Phys. Rev. D 21 (1980) 2291; J. Fröhlich and T. Spencer,
  Commun. Math. Phys. 83 (1982) 411: massless Coulomb phase of 4D compact
  $U(1)$ at weak coupling.
- D. J. Gross and F. Wilczek, Phys. Rev. Lett. 30 (1973) 1343;
  H. D. Politzer, Phys. Rev. Lett. 30 (1973) 1346: asymptotic freedom, the
  one-loop running quoted for transmutation.
- V. Ivrii, "100 years of Weyl's law", Bull. Math. Sci. 6 (2016) 379: Weyl
  asymptotics cited for the classical-limit level density.
