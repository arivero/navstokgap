## Findings

Vasubandhu's exact lines `ṣaṭkena yugapad yogāt ... ṣaṇṇāṃ samānadeśatvāt
piṇḍaḥ syād aṇumātrakaḥ // 12 //` support the static dilemma: six directional
unions imply six parts; common location collapses the aggregate to atom-size.
Kārikā 13 rejects the escape that only aggregates unite; 14 tests directional
parts and shielding. Translation uncertainty is chiefly *yoga/saṃyoga* (union,
junction, not necessarily tactile “touch”) and *digbhāga* (directional part,
not automatically Euclidean side).

Nāgārjuna II.1–3 exactly contrasts *gata*, *agata*, *gamyamāna* and motion/
going. It is a predicate analysis, not a positive kinematics model. The source
idea is to keep static distinct-contact composition separate from the ordered
trajectory/arrow question; the texts do not establish an action scale.
