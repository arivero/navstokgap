## Mathematical and literature status

For compact convex centrally symmetric `U`, the feasible central fibre `{u in U: |Au|<=eps}` has scalar radius `r_L`. Two inputs compatible with one record have half-difference in this fibre, so their outputs differ by at most `2r_L`; the midpoint of the compatible scalar interval has error at most `r_L`. An extremizer `u` and `-u`, with opposite record errors, share the zero record and force the converse lower bound.

The explicit bounded-acceleration pair in `notes/indistinguishable-phase-bound.md` has common initial state and identical entire noisy history; its terminal momentum error is `2 sqrt(mF eps)` (terminal separation is twice this). This is a derived mechanical specialization of the source's indistinguishability argument.
