## Match to R07 and limits

Liberzon matches the normalized bounded-input double-integrator structure and supports the prior-art label “bang--bang extremals,” not the exact fixed-time image of the linear map `f ↦ (∫(ℓ-s)f/m, ∫f)`. R07's one-switch profiles follow directly by a bathtub/rearrangement argument at fixed impulse; the lens `|z-d/2|≤(1-d²)/4` and area `2F²ℓ³/(3m)` are derived specializations. The source's acceleration bound is dimensionless (`m=1` in effect), with no physical mass or force units, no delayed-record information model, and no canonical-area/action interpretation.

The `+F/-F` argument is an elementary deterministic two-point indistinguishability proof: identical accessible records plus endpoint separation imply one of two absolute errors is at least half the separation. The statistical Le Cam lead was not accessible, so no probabilistic observation law, testing distance, or constant is imported. In particular, R07 must not claim a statistical minimax theorem or ontic noise result.

Missing assumptions to retain explicitly: force is an arbitrary `L∞` input with both signs; the observer has no post-zero force/state record; endpoints are fixed-time; no force probability law is assumed; and any speed ceiling requires the separate condition `Fℓ/m<c` (after choosing `p0`). Cooling or preparation restrictions are not supplied by the sources. The positive scale therefore closes as `F→0` or `ℓ→0`; it is not universal without a physical lower bound on the force-delay product.
