# Source ideas for the next calculation

Select by proof obligation, then read the linked companion. These cards route
existing bibliography into model design; the ledger and task board carry the
live acceptance and priority records. Proposed tests below are research actions.

## Memory, observability and a mechanical receiver — A09/G02

**Source construction:** Pavliotis converts an integrated velocity correlation
into a generator Poisson equation. Read the [source companion](../docs/Pavliotis_GreenKubo_2010.md),
§2, pp. 4–6, especially the visually checked p. 5.
The [gap note](../notes/susceptibility-gap.md) applies it to observable response.

**Use next:** specify a conservative receiver and a measurement map; determine
which slow modes its velocity sees. Track the variance, correlation time and
readout constants under weak coupling. This tests whether the positive plateau
has a mechanical origin and whether a response bound controls the full gap.
The finite-state inverse-generator construction is the source ingredient;
uniform mechanical access is the additional proof obligation.

## Composition versus preparation — A09 obligation 4

**Source construction:** Green–Kubo response and Gaussian bridge covariance
provide two settings for a constituent-to-centre covariance calculation. Read
[B16's companion](../docs/batches/B16/composition-source-companion.md), with
Pavliotis p. 5 and Pitman–Yor p. 6 as its exact anchors, then the
[composition note](../notes/composition-universality.md).

**Use next:** vary the reservoir energy or interaction rate while preserving
mass-composition closure. Ask which classical premise equates the resulting
coefficients across preparations. Mass additivity is a repository consequence;
the cited sources supply covariance constructions rather than a universality axiom.

## Coherent cuts and a physical action parameter — A09 obligation 5

**Source construction:** Skopenkov–Ustinov's normalized corner weights and
two-component recurrence conserve finite-step probability. Read
[B18's companion](../docs/batches/B18/checkerboard-recurrence-source-companion.md),
Definition 2 and Propositions 5–6, pp. 12–13; the source's point-source
continuum caveat is on p. 19. The [checkerboard note](../notes/checkerboard-dynamics.md)
contains the maintained wavepacket and measured-cut calculations.

**Use next:** identify a physical composition premise for unobserved cuts and
track where it introduces amplitudes and their dimensional phase scale. Test
the same premise against a real telegraph model and repeated measurements.
This connects a known quantum construction to the still-open selection gate.

## A shared observable across regulators — M06/B07b

**Source construction:** Wilson–Kogut compare cutoff models at a common
physical correlation length. Read [the companion](../docs/WilsonKogut_1974.md)
for §12.2 coverage, then [the two-regulator audit](../notes/two-regulator-audit.md).

**Use next:** specify what measured quantity is held fixed as cuts are inserted,
and compute which mechanical coefficients must run. Test whether force/energy
control allows the resulting family. A fixed positive observable used to set
the running parameter is an input; selection requires an independent physical
condition. B07b's tangent-groupoid theorem remains a separate source task.

## Newton's limiting geometry and historical conjectures — B11/H02/H05

**Source construction:** Newton's first and last ratios support his limiting
geometry. Read the [Section I companion](../docs/Newton_Principia_BookI_SectionI_Motte1729_Wilkins2002.md)
and the task's exact passage; use the
[constant-force note](../notes/principia-constant-force-action.md) for modern
area/action calculations. For manuscript claims use the
[NATP00385 audit](../notes/newton-NATP00385-audit.md).

**Use next:** identify the uniformity assumption in the chosen shrinking-area
argument and test it in a specified force-controlled path family. Historical
attribution requires a dated passage; the proposed action-scale reading is a
modern conjecture. Cone-reception and I003 drafts keep their separate source
and proof gates in the task board.
