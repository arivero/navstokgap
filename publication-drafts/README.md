# Publication manuscript drafts A–E

These manuscripts were prepared from the repository's maintained proof notes in response to the publication-plan request. A–D are full working drafts. E is a complete journal-format author-review manuscript directed to *Foundations of Physics* or a comparable foundations journal; author names, affiliations, and corresponding-author details await human approval.

All five manuscripts remain internal drafts. In particular, the literature-positioning and independent theorem audits specified in [`publication-routes/04-gauge-benchmark-publication-plan.md`](../publication-routes/04-gauge-benchmark-publication-plan.md) are prerequisites to external submission. No author identity, affiliation, contribution, or journal approval has been invented.
