# A sharp classical lower bound on orbit action

Publication assessment, 2026-09-13. The cleanest short result identified in
the general review; saved as a route for consideration, rather than a change
to the active research queue.

## The candidate

C060–C061 establish, for regular closed trajectories with $p=m\dot q$, speed
floor $v_*>0$ and force ceiling $F_{\max}$,

$$
J=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint p\cdot dq
\;\ge\;
\frac{m^2v_*^3}{F_{\max}},
$$

with equality attained by a circular orbit in a smooth force-bounded potential.

Its appeal is the connection between geometry and mechanics: total turning
supplies $2\pi$, bounded force limits momentum turning, and persistent motion
supplies the action cost. The relativistic extension fits naturally.

## Journal route and remaining check

I would consider *Total curvature and the minimum action of a closed orbit*
as an AJP Note candidate.
[The proof](../notes/closed-orbit-force-action.md) is compact and essentially
self-contained. Its publication case depends on whether this consequence of
Fenchel's theorem—and its sharp mechanical realization—provides an original
teaching insight. [B32](../references/batches/B32.md) checked the geometric
ingredient, rather than establishing originality of the resulting inequality.

AJP welcomes illuminating connections and original physical insight, while
requiring accessibility and teaching value. See the
[AJP editorial policy](https://www.aapt.org/Publications/AJP/About/editorial_policy.cfm).

The general review recommends retaining this as a short-note alternative to
the [elastic-gas paper](01-elastic-gas-comparison.md), with a dedicated
originality audit before submission. The
[classical-spin construction](03-classical-spin-closure.md) is the companion
foundations candidate.
