# A13: ordered-beam preparation

The maintained proof is in [the collision manuscript](../papers/collision-action-relaxation.tex),
§6, “Ordered streams at fixed mechanical scales”; see its
[PDF](../out/papers/collision-action-relaxation.pdf).

C051 gives a stationary two-stream preparation with periodic tagged motion
and zero long-window response at A12's mass, speed, density and mean collision
frequency. The comparison changes joint position–velocity correlations,
including mark independence. The proof uses uniform Palm residual time and
exact cancellation over a full period.

[B26](../references/batches/B26.md) records bounded unmatched prior-art
coverage; [the review](../reviews/ordered-preparation-B26.md) records proof
acceptance. [A13 handoff](../research/handoffs/A13.md) routes the next renewal
preparation test. This note routes to the maintained proof rather than copying it.
