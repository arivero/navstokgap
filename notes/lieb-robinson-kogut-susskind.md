# A Lieb--Robinson bound for the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian, with velocity $\propto Nc/g^2$

The Hamiltonian of [the obligations map](mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md)
has exactly the structure for which a Lieb--Robinson bound survives an
unbounded generator: its unbounded part is a sum of commuting
single-link terms, and its interaction is a bounded finite-range sum of
plaquette terms. Passing to the interaction picture generated by the
electric term leaves a time-dependent bounded interaction of unchanged
norm and range, so the standard theorem applies and gives, for
observables $A$ on a set $X$ of links and $B$ on $Y$,
$$\big\|[A(t),B]\big\|\ \le\ 2\|A\|\,\|B\|\,|X|\;
e^{-\big(d(X,Y)-v|t|\big)/a},\qquad
v\ =\ 2e\,a\,J/\hbar\ =\ 32\,e\,\frac{\dim(G)_{\rm f}\,c}{g^2},$$
where $J=\max_\ell\sum_{p\ni\ell}\|w_p\|=16N\hbar c/(g^2a)$ for $SU(N)$ in
the fundamental normalization, so $v\le C\,Nc/g^2$ with $C$ a pure
number, **uniform in the lattice size and in the volume**. Three
consequences. Combined with T2 and the Hastings--Koma and
Nachtergaele--Sims clustering theorems, the strong-coupling phase has
exponential decay of correlations with correlation length
$\xi\le v\hbar/\Delta\simeq C'aN/(\gamma C_2\,g^4)$, shrinking as $g^4$
grows. The velocity diverges as $g\to0$, so the lattice light cone opens
faster than $c$ at weak coupling, and the recovery of relativistic
causality is one more statement that holds only along the scaling curve.
And the bound supplies clustering while leaving the openness of the
gapped set of [the critical-coupling note](gapped-set-critical-coupling.md)
open, because the available stability theorems additionally require
frustration-freeness, which the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian lacks. The
reduction to the standard theorem is the content here; the standard
theorem is cited. Constants explicit; nothing promoted.

## 1. The structure that makes the unbounded term harmless

Take the links as the sites of the lattice and write, as in
[the obligations map](mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md) §1,
$$H=H_{\rm E}+W,\qquad
H_{\rm E}=\frac{\hbar c\,g^2}{2a}\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal E}(-\Delta_\ell),\qquad
W=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}w_p,\quad
w_p=\frac{2\hbar c}{a\,g^2}\big(N-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}U_p\big).$$

Three properties are all that the argument uses.

1. *The unbounded part is single-site and commuting.* $-\Delta_\ell$ acts
   on the single factor $L^2(G)$ of link $\ell$, and the terms for
   different links commute. Each generates a one-parameter unitary group
   $e^{-isc g^2\Delta_\ell/(2a)}$ whose adjoint action preserves the
   algebra of that link.
2. *The interaction is bounded.* $0\le w_p\le\frac{4N\hbar c}{a g^2}$ in
   operator norm for $SU(N)$, since
   $|\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}U|\le N$; for a general compact $G$
   in a faithful representation of dimension $\dim_{\rm f}$ replace $N$ by
   $\dim_{\rm f}$.
3. *The range is one plaquette.* Each $w_p$ acts on the four links of
   $p$; a given link lies in exactly four plaquettes in three spatial
   dimensions (two planes containing its direction, two plaquettes on
   either side in each). Hence
   $$J:=\max_{\ell}\sum_{p\ni\ell}\|w_p\|\ \le\ 4\cdot\frac{4N\hbar c}{a g^2}
   =\frac{16N\hbar c}{a\,g^2}.$$

## 2. The reduction and the bound

**Proposition 1.** Let $\tau_t(A)=e^{itH/\hbar}Ae^{-itH/\hbar}$. For $A$
supported on a set $X$ of links and $B$ on a disjoint set $Y$, with
$d(X,Y)$ the lattice distance,
$$\big\|[\tau_t(A),B]\big\|\ \le\ 2\|A\|\,\|B\|\,|X|\,
\exp\!\Big[\frac{v|t|-d(X,Y)}{a}\Big],\qquad v=\frac{2e\,aJ}{\hbar}\le\frac{32\,e\,Nc}{g^2}.$$

*Proof.* Let $U_{\rm E}(t)=e^{-itH_{\rm E}/\hbar}$ and define the
interaction-picture interaction
$\tilde w_p(t)=U_{\rm E}(t)^*w_pU_{\rm E}(t)$. Since $H_{\rm E}$ is a sum
of commuting single-link terms, $U_{\rm E}(t)$ factorizes over links and
its adjoint action preserves each link algebra, so $\tilde w_p(t)$ is
supported on the same four links and $\|\tilde w_p(t)\|=\|w_p\|$ for all
$t$. The full evolution factorizes as
$e^{-itH/\hbar}=U_{\rm E}(t)\,\tilde U(t)$ with $\tilde U$ generated by
the time-dependent bounded interaction $\tilde W(t)=\sum_p\tilde w_p(t)$,
and conjugation by $U_{\rm E}(t)$ preserves supports and norms. The
Lieb--Robinson theorem for time-dependent bounded finite-range
interactions (Lieb--Robinson, Commun. Math. Phys. 28 (1972) 251;
Nachtergaele--Sims, Commun. Math. Phys. 265 (2006) 119, in the form used
for the clustering theorem; metadata level) applies to $\tilde W$ with
the constant $J$ of Section 1, and transferring back through
$U_{\rm E}$ gives the statement with the same constants. $\square$

The same argument is the one used for oscillator lattices, where the
unbounded on-site harmonic part is treated exactly and the bounded
anharmonic interaction carries the velocity (Nachtergaele--Raz--Schlein--Sims,
Commun. Math. Phys. 286 (2008) 1073; metadata level). The gauge
constraint plays no role: the bound holds on the full Hilbert space and
restricts to the physical subspace, which is invariant under $H$.

**Remark (what fails without the structure).** If the electric term
coupled two links, its unboundedness would enter the iteration and no
bound of this form would follow. The single-link form of $\sum_a E^a_\ell E^a_\ell$
is therefore load-bearing, and it is the same feature that made the
strong-coupling hypothesis check of
[the T2 note](strong-coupling-uniform-gap.md) work.

## 3. Correlation length at strong coupling

**Corollary 2.** For $g\ge g_0(G)$, the infinite-volume ground state of
the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian has exponentially decaying connected
correlations of local gauge-invariant observables, with correlation
length
$$\xi\ \le\ \frac{C\,v\,\hbar}{\Delta}\ \le\ \frac{C'\,a\,N}{\gamma\,C_2(R_{\min})\,g^4},$$
for pure numbers $C,C'$.

*Proof.* Proposition 1 supplies the Lieb--Robinson bound;
[the T2 note](strong-coupling-uniform-gap.md) supplies the uniform gap
$\Delta\ge\gamma(g^2/2)C_2\hbar c/a$ and a unique ground state. The
exponential clustering theorem for gapped local Hamiltonians
(Nachtergaele--Sims, Commun. Math. Phys. 265 (2006) 119;
Hastings--Koma, Commun. Math. Phys. 265 (2006) 781; both metadata level)
gives decay with length of order $v\hbar/\Delta$. Substituting
$v\le32eNc/g^2$ and $\Delta\ge\gamma C_2g^2\hbar c/(2a)$ gives the
displayed form. $\square$

The correlation length shrinks like $g^{-4}$ as the coupling grows,
which matches the strong-coupling expansion, where the leading
correlation of two plaquettes at distance $d$ costs a tube of
$\sim d/a$ plaquettes each suppressed by $g^{-4}$. Yarotsky's conclusion
3, used in [the T2 note](strong-coupling-uniform-gap.md), already
asserts exponential clustering; Corollary 2 adds the explicit length in
terms of the gap and the velocity.

## 4. The velocity at weak coupling, and lattice causality

The velocity $v\le32eNc/g^2$ exceeds $c$ once $g^2<32eN$, that is
throughout the weak-coupling regime relevant to the continuum limit.
The lattice theory has a light cone set by the interaction strength
rather than by the Minkowski metric, and the two agree only after the
scaling limit restores relativistic causality. This is one more
obligation in the same family as T3 and T4: the axioms require exact
locality of the continuum theory, and here the lattice bound alone
degrades as $g\to0$. It also gives a second reading of the crossover of
[the valley note](torus-valley-potential.md): the lattice light cone
crosses a box of side $L$ in time $L/v\sim Lg^2/(Nc)$, shorter than the
inverse zero-mode gap $L/(g^{2/3}c)$ whenever $g^{4/3}<1/N$, so at weak
coupling the whole box is causally connected on the timescale of the
gap, and the small-volume description is consistent.

## 5. What the bound supplies, and what openness still needs

Lieb--Robinson bounds enter gap-stability theorems as one hypothesis
among several. The available theorems for perturbations of gapped local
Hamiltonians, including the topological-order stability results used in
[the T2 note](strong-coupling-uniform-gap.md), additionally require the
unperturbed Hamiltonian to be frustration-free with a locally
topologically ordered ground space. The Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian is
frustration-free only at $g=\infty$, where the ground state is the
constant function annihilated by every $-\Delta_\ell$; at finite $g$ the
electric and magnetic terms compete and no state minimizes both. So
openness of the gapped set needs either a stability theorem for
non-frustration-free Hamiltonians with a uniform gap, or an argument
specific to gauge theory. Proposition 1 removes one of the two obstacles
that stood in the way, and names the remaining one precisely.

## 6. Consequence for STATE

The Lieb--Robinson question raised in
[the critical-coupling note](gapped-set-critical-coupling.md) §5 is
settled: the bound holds with velocity $32eNc/g^2$ uniformly in volume,
because the unbounded part of the Hamiltonian is single-link and
commuting. Openness of the gapped set now rests on a single missing
ingredient, a stability theorem that does not assume frustration-freeness.
Two further items follow from the bound itself: the explicit
strong-coupling correlation length of Corollary 2, and the observation
that the lattice light cone opens faster than $c$ at weak coupling, which
belongs with T3 and T4 as a statement that holds only along the scaling
curve.
