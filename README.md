The two open goals are a physically justified positive action scale for
Newtonian completion and continuum pure $SU(3)$ Yang–Mills with a finite positive
mass gap. The [pion comparison](notes/three-continuum-limits.md) tests which
symmetries and spectral weights a proposed mechanism preserves. Classical
existence, conditional action floors, quantum structure and identification with
physical $\hbar$ remain different claims.

The [dependency paths](research/dependencies.md) route the arguments;
[obstructions](research/obstructions.md) preserve countertests and exact failure
scopes. Former note URLs redirect to canonical sections. Internal model reviews
and retrieval priorities confer neither independent peer review nor novelty.

Read [AGENTS](AGENTS.md) and [LLM](LLM.md) before working. New research is
suspended during consolidation. Preserve unrelated changes and use written
mathematical derivations. Build a changed scientific note with
`make paper NOTE=<slug>`, then `make site` and `make check` for document,
catalog, link and source-checksum integrity. Routine full paper builds and
numerical/symbolic verification scripts are prohibited. The landing diagrams
illustrate refinement; they are not simulations or proofs.

The [generated sitemap](https://arivero.github.io/navstokgap/sitemap.xml) follows
the site manifest; building sends no external notifications. The repository
contains primary-source companions, independently useful manuscripts and dated
research history. Their original claims and next-task wording do not override
maintained arguments or STATE. Commits/pushes are authorized; external submissions
and correspondence need separate direction.
