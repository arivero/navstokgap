The common method

Add detail. Compare. Control the limit.

  1. ＋

    Insert a new piece

    Add an intermediate time to a motion, or an edge to a lattice.

  2. ⇄

    Compare descriptions

    Find which observations and interactions survive removing the added variable.

  3. ∞

    Prove what survives

    Control the errors through unlimited refinement and test whether a physical scale remains.
