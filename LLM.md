# What this repository establishes

Read [AGENTS](AGENTS.md) for work rules and [STATE](research/STATE.md) for the
live direction. New research is suspended during the scientific consolidation.
The [catalog](notes/index.md) and [typed JSON links](research/note-index.json)

its assumptions before reusing a catalog statement. A model's ACCEPT/REFINE or
correctness score is an internal review, not independent peer review, and
establishes no literature priority.

## Four separate claims

1. **Classical existence/completion.** The [polygon construction](notes/balaban-ode-lesson.md)
gives causal motion under stated regularity. Its logarithmic counterexamples
separate unbounded slope from actual finite-time mergers. Enlarged
[cusp histories](notes/newton-cusp-preparation-floor.md) retain classical
branch information. Existence of a quantum family does not exclude those
classical completions.
2. **Conditional floors.** [Cusp preparation laws](notes/newton-cusp-preparation-floor.md),
[specified Gaussian marks](notes/mark-cost-and-statistical-floor.md) and
[response ceilings](notes/newton-branch-response-action.md) have different
hypotheses. Their positive constants are not automatically the same observable
or the same physical scale. Classical density/entropy witnesses and complete
apparatus records must accompany these implications.
3. **Quantum structure.** The [bounded lifting theorem](notes/lipschitz-certificate-quantum-lift.md)
constructs a noncommutative theory for a declared conservative $C^{1,1}$ class
using a named analytic toolkit. The universal lifting conjecture remains open.
[General instrument bounds](notes/record-distance-path-length.md) supply Weyl/
Hilbert kinematics. A covariance inequality or a classical branch probability
alone supplies neither quantum states nor quantum instruments.
4. **Universality and calibration.** [Dimensional units](notes/action-unit-dimensional-selection.md),
[mechanical/algebraic composition and spin phases](notes/rotation-composition-universality.md),
and [classical noise composition](notes/composition-universality.md) retain
distinct physical premises.
A common coefficient can still be zero or have arbitrary supplied magnitude.
No universal measured physical $\hbar$ has been derived from Newtonian completion.

[Classical detector events](notes/local-detector-coincidences.md) preserve the
passive-barrier and shared-release countermodels beside the independent-monotone
coincidence bound. [CHSH](notes/shared-readiness-chsh.md) uses different setting
and locality premises; neither dimensionless witness calibrates physical $\hbar$.

## Corrections that change a theorem

- [Low-dimensional models](notes/low-dimensional-mass-gap.md) distinguish a
classical area-restricted sublevel set from a quantum spectral gap. The invariant
sector can have a different first gap; a supercritical inverse-square extension
has a two-sided negative tower and no ground state.
- [Three-dimensional scaling](notes/three-dimensional-gap-one-function.md)
requires nonzero-mode control before assigning the constant-mode asymptotic to
the full physical theory. Dimensional covariance and local stochastic time
evolution supply no such spectral transport.
- The [one-loop valley](notes/torus-valley-potential.md#4-saturation-and-central-holonomies)
has bounded periodic full potential, but subtracting its linear zero-mode term
does not preserve those properties on the cover. A local quadratic coefficient
does not prove the global H3 gap or relative bound in the
[Feshbach reduction](notes/weak-coupling-feshbach-reduction.md).

- [Independent boxes and Gaussian marks](notes/mark-cost-and-statistical-floor.md)
use different noise assumptions. Marginal hard widths do not establish an
independent feasible box. A zero lower product bound alone does not provide
arbitrarily accurate balanced protocols. Compact support of a state's momentum
law does not establish a noncommuting instrument's impulse width.
- [Aperture, recoil and disturbance](notes/record-distance-path-length.md)
need domains and bounds on all translated/hybrid conditional states. Endpoint
resources alone are insufficient; preparation-independent cell thresholds fail
without such resources. Split-resource optimization needs common envelopes.
- [Cut energy and phase](notes/cut-measure-newton.md) have exact constants
but different physical assumptions. Mark-information attainability for a general
force uses the one-sign construction. Coherent control and copy/pass resources
price phase readout; one radian is not an action quantum.
- Newton's fits are longitudinal periodic dispositions. The
[historical manuscript](notes/planck-gap-paper.md) states the extra continuous-speed
interpretation and quantum calibration explicitly. Fits do not imply transverse
uncertainty, the Weyl relations or a universal positive $\hbar$.
- [Classical apparatus](notes/autonomous-finite-readout.md),
[calibration](notes/calibration-tolerance-recovery.md) and
[force geometry](notes/causal-force-information.md) keep initial preparation,
retained records, calibrated parameters, budgets and limiting order explicit.
A reconstruction-error product is not a covariance action by definition.
- [Coherent-stream limits](notes/zero-branch-reachability.md) need the exact
frequency-sum criterion as well as their coherence premise. Equal-action cross
terms can cancel; derivative sign changes alone do not ensure three illuminated
branches. Gaussian finite-record laws are continuous, not constant, in action.
The angle model uses signed image width: a compressing Kepler gradient cannot
wrap before folding; its dephasing scale is not an unconditional first-overlap time. The incoherent packet attachment recovers the classical swarm for
quadratic forces. Fixed-interval [Coulomb recovery](notes/self-sufficiency-contrast.md)
is distinct from collision continuation and extension/domain selection.
- [Radiation/depot/thermal tests](notes/necessity-unit-and-indeterminacy.md)
distinguish normalized spectral shape, free amplitude, supplied feedback scale
and a work lower bound. A lower bound is not an attainable optimum; cross-field
thermal universality remains conjectural.
- Gauge gaps name their operator. The Wilson polymer and Dobrushin sufficient
regions are explicit; the [KS uniform numerical threshold](notes/strong-coupling-uniform-gap.md#continuous-time-histories-and-the-corrected-overlap-criterion)
was withdrawn. Existential strong-coupling volume uniformity survives. Positivity
at every bare coupling is stronger than the weak scaling trajectory requires.
- [Flow conjugation](notes/flow-conjugation-truncation.md) preserves spectra and
multiplication norms. [Auxiliary Gaussian locality](notes/flow-conjugation-truncation.md#exact-continuum-variation-and-its-gauge-convention)
is not a Hamiltonian relative bound; the full Landau growth is $gB-k_\parallel^2$,
not $2gB$. The actual gauge-modified Jacobian has extra terms.
- [Large-field action/model counts](notes/large-field-action-lower-bound.md)
prove no normalized entropy or rarity bound. Block $L^2$ thresholds, supremum
curvature, spectral field strength and real/complex multiplicities differ.
The [signed operator comparison](notes/large-field-operator-inequality.md)
retains conditional localization assumptions and diagnoses one construction.
- [Agmon](notes/ground-state-measure-transfer.md#weighted-estimates-and-global-magnetic-tails) controls the global excess;
[ground-state transfer](notes/ground-state-measure-transfer.md) requires matching
the anisotropic measure and projector limits. [Spectral upper bounds](notes/finiteness-half-flowed-susceptibility.md)
require admissible Hamiltonian insertions and infrared moments; four-dimensional
flow UV finiteness alone supplies neither. A channel edge can miss the global
gap. All inverse moments may be finite at edge zero, although their complete
sequence can determine that channel's edge.
- [Gaussian blocking](notes/small-field-step-gaussian.md) keeps two-link angle
normalization and infinity-norm shell counting consistent. Hard conditioning,
soft-constraint decay and the conditional interacting remainder are distinct.
- [Semisimple Gauss law](notes/lattice-gap-upper-bounds.md#semisimple-gauss-law-and-the-limits-of-the-dressing-argument)
excludes constant-colour electric trials. The former universal string-cost
claim for every field-dependent dressed trial was not proved.
- [Compact-group images](notes/sun-midpoint-centre.md) are signed Cartan-image
expansions; a positive winding mixture is proved for $U(1)$ only. Flat holonomies
remain in the [mid-plane action](notes/su2-midplane-order-t.md); plaquette fluxes
alone do not parametrize a torus. Barriered weak-strip estimates do not establish
full recoupling or depth-uniform generated-action matching.

## Units, sources and artifacts

Keep physical $\hbar$ explicit, $h_P=2\pi\hbar$, and
$\epsilon=\hbar/S_0$ dimensionless. Mesh, record resolution, flow time, bare
phase regulator and lattice spacing have their own units and limiting orders.
A finite-box, auxiliary-clock or Markov relaxation rate is not automatically a
physical Hamiltonian mass gap. The [refinement correspondence](research/refinement-correspondences.md)
labels actual maps separately from analogies.

Primary material remains in [source companions](docs/README.md) and the
[bibliography](references/library.bib). The claim-specific reading label
(metadata, abstract, passage or full-read) determines what was checked; a DOI
lookup alone validates no theorem or historical interpretation. The
[current obstruction map](research/obstructions.md) routes to the actual
countermodels and corrected claims. Retired internal scores and handoffs
remain only in git history; short catalog phrases record completed work.
[Papers](research/pdf-audit.md) distinguishes generated current treatments,
independent manuscripts and historical snapshots. Build only the changed note;
[AGENTS](AGENTS.md) and `make check` enforce document integrity without mathematical
verification scripts. Git history supplies editorial provenance.
