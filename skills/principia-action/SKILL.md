# Choose the conclusion a calculation can change

Read [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), [STATE](../../research/STATE.md) and the
selected [catalog entry](../../notes/index.md). Follow
[poormanrag](../poormanrag/SKILL.md) for actual proof, correction and
source retrieval. Do not infer a task from the newest filename, an old
task number or a retired handoff.

When research is authorized, identify the physical premise, construction
or limiting estimate that the proposed work can change. Compare plausible
mechanisms before extending one solvable model. State its admitted states,
observations and dynamics; give a decisive countertest. A new apparatus
parameter or sharper constant needs a reason tied to that question.

For action selection distinguish definition/units, exclusion of zero,
convergence, universality and identification with quantum phase or
commutation structure. Test quiet preparations, admitted dilations and
complete-record classical models. Positive excitation or a supplied
reference fixes a scale only within its stated class.

For a gap specify operator, domain, physical or auxiliary clock, observable
access and units. Track every constant through volume and cutoff limits.
A sampling rate, an energy gap and a finite positive physical continuum
spectral edge need different identifications.

For the historical Newton comparison, use the selected edition and
[geometry proof](../../notes/newton-insertion-action.md#the-source-geometry-and-three-different-areas).
Galileo's inertial line and falling parabola enclose twice the
matched-endpoint chord lens. Kepler sectors are a different observable.
The necessity question asks which independently justified premise excludes
classical refinement; a supplied physical $\hbar$ gives a benchmark.
Newton's quantity of motion is momentum. Modern fluxional-time and
quantum interpretations remain interpretations.

Recover a source as passage, borrowed construction, proposed use and
decisive premise. Distinguish the author's conclusion from the repository's
inference. Reuse recorded reading coverage and access limitations; open
a borrowed proof for a concrete suspected error affecting a result in use.
A bounded search establishes neither exhaustive coverage nor novelty.

Integrate useful proofs and corrections into their maintained argument.
Record completed work in one short catalog phrase. Keep STATE current.
Source companions retain actual source evidence; git retains retired
handoffs, status snapshots and internal reviews. Do not recreate those
files, model scores or a parallel queue. Numerical or symbolic mathematical
verification scripts remain prohibited; targeted document builds and
integrity checks remain permitted under AGENTS.
