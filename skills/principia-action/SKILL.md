## Recover the selected task's sources

On a fresh research session, or when source-to-task context has been lost:

1. Read STATE, PROGRAMME and the selected TASKS entry. Reuse their contents
   and a current source capsule already retained in context.
2. Read the relevant card in references/IDEA_BRIDGES.md and its source
   companion, including reading coverage and access gaps. Add a second source
   only when it supplies a complementary premise or countertest. Follow the
   linked derivation when doing mathematical work.
3. Retain a compact capsule: **source and passage → borrowed construction →
   proposed task test → decisive premise or limit**. Attribute the source's
   statement separately from the repository's inference.

Recovery uses existing companions; it does not launch a literature campaign.
A source worker may use the supplied capsule within its assigned budget and
returns one source-to-model suggestion. Save useful connections in the handoff;
update IDEA_BRIDGES only when they change a future decision. A remembered
formula or title remains a discovery lead until checked.

## Route to the smallest relevant argument

- **Quantum necessity and compatibility:** notes/quantum-exclusion-premises.md
  and the selected Q01/K01 card. State preparations, effects, composition and
  transformations before testing the classical alternative. Keep finite
  statistical dimension, distinguishability capacity, microscopic reversibility
  and operational closure separate. For dimensional comparison use
  notes/checkerboard-dynamics.md; identify its supplied amplitudes, measurement
  rule and action constant. Do not substitute an old B01 capsule for newer
  task-specific audits linked by the current card.
- **Action selection or universality:** research/ACTION_FIELD_TARGET.md and
  notes/composition-universality.md. Track definition/units, exclusion of zero,
  convergence, universality and quantum identification separately. Locate any
  positive reference or excitation premise at the step where it is used.
- **Receiver or bath:** papers/classical-action-field.tex or
  papers/collision-action-relaxation.tex. Trace variance and correlation time
  to preparation, energy and interaction inputs; test quiet and rapidly
  decorrelating families inside the admitted class.
- **Cut refinement or finite speed:** research/CUT_POINT_TARGET.md, then
  notes/bridge-crossover.md or notes/bounded-acceleration-return.md. Distinguish
  sampling cuts, conditioned bridges and physical interventions, with their
  force, mass and speed bounds.
- **Gap estimates:** notes/susceptibility-gap.md and the selected gap card.
  Specify operator, domain, invariant law, observable access and clock. Track
  constants through the claimed limits; converting a rate to an energy requires
  an independently identified action factor and physical dynamics.
- **Consolidation:** notes/action-scale-obstructions.md and claims/LEDGER.md.
  Reuse accepted proofs and audits with a claim-to-source map and consistency
  review. A new mathematical result still needs the protocol's source audit.
- **Principia or historical interpretation:** notes/principia-constant-force-action.md
  and the edition's source companion. Use Lemmas X–XI/projectile Scholium for
  constant-force geometry, Proposition I for swept areas. Newton's quantity of
  motion is momentum; an implicit h→0 reading is a modern conjecture. The 1846
  HTML has image equations and modern descriptions; NATP00385's diplomatic/XML
  versions expose revisions. Attribute these layers and retain folio anchors.

## Keep the conclusion tied to its premises

Name the varying parameter: physical time, mesh resolution, observation window,
action parameter, volume or relativistic limit. A stationary long-window
plateau does not establish physical-time attraction. A positive endpoint cost,
an infimum of positive excess costs and an operator gap are different objects.

Keep proof status in the ledger and literature coverage in source companions;
a bounded search establishes neither exhaustive coverage nor novelty. Source
acquisition follows references/SOURCE_POLICY.md; use physics-source-downloads
when retrieving originals or making companions. Numerical/symbolic verification
scripts remain prohibited under AGENTS.md; document/source tooling is allowed.
Delegation and checkpoints follow agents/PROTOCOL.md and existing authorization.
