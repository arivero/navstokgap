# I001: from vanishing areas to an action scale

Status: user-originated heuristic. Recorded 2026-09-05.

The proposal is that an additional action-like degree of freedom underlies
Newtonian trajectory limits, with Feynman's phase providing a regularization
that produces a physical bound. Four concrete interpretations give it testable
forms.

| Interpretation | Object to specify | First test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trajectory fluctuation | Variation $\eta$ and quadratic action cost | Scale the amplitude and compare cost with the normalized Jacobi spectrum |
| Additional field | Field space, coupling and measure for $S[q,\chi]$ | Eliminate $\chi$ and identify the origin of each effective action scale |
| Operational resolution | States, phase encoding, resources and confidence | Derive the action threshold and its copy-count dependence |
| Distributional constraint | Kernel, normalization, test functions and limit | Compare the free-kernel delta limit with the limit of its spatial derivative |

The first calculation gives action costs approaching zero under amplitude scaling.
The quantum two-arm calculation gives a positive threshold at fixed resources.
These results make the admissible states and operational comparison the next
choices in constructing a gap mechanism.
