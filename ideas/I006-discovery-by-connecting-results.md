# I006: discovery by connecting established results

Origin: the user's 2026-09-10 observation that discovery may fill the regions
between established approaches, rather than only extend their frontier.
Treat the convex-envelope image as a research heuristic. The mathematical
work is to build valid connections between results and their premises.

## A completed connection

[R11](../notes/indistinguishable-phase-bound.md) joins three existing tools:
bounded-force reachability, symmetric convex optimal recovery, and robust
differentiation. It gives a sharp classical information gap that survives dense
observed cuts at fixed error tolerance. [B42](../references/batches/B42.md)
identifies the exact established construction, preventing a change of notation
from being presented as discovery.

R12 completed the third-row test: [product minimax radii](../notes/minimax-composition.md)
give an extensive identical-copy action product and quantify the cost of losing
constituent records. A08's invariant variance law does not transfer to this
observable. R13 now tests the finite-horizon boundary, with known initial data
and too little time to prepare R11's extremal pair.

## Working rule

Before extending a solvable example, name the connection it advances. Write
the source and destination observables, their assumptions, the transformation,
and a decisive compatibility test. A source batch should return a reusable
argument and its missing premise, alongside the prior-art status. Keep a
promising but unproved connection in the idea register until the argument
closes; preserve failed tests as information about the required premises.
