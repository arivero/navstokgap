# I007: static compatibility before a temporal cut

**Question:** can physically motivated rules for combining local measurement
descriptions force more structure than a classical joint state, and where
would those rules acquire an action-valued scale?

Origin: the user's 2026-09-10 suggestion to revisit Indian, Chinese and
Persianate cone/arrow arguments and consider statics alongside measurement.
[H10's synthesis](../notes/static-composition-classics.md) supplies the source
connections. Status: research proposal, no new ledger claim.

## A bounded test — K01

1. Choose a finite family of measurement arrangements on one classical
   system plus apparatus. Specify the permitted preparations, responses,
   overlaps and repeatability conditions at a fixed time.
2. Ask whether compatible descriptions on the overlaps extend to a common
   state or distribution. Allow apparatus variables explicitly; mark any
   assumption that an outcome is independent of arrangement.
3. Compare a classical global-state construction with one primary quantum
   contextuality construction. Read the hypotheses of the original
   Kochen–Specker paper before importing its obstruction; it is presently
   a bibliography lead, not a proof reviewed by H10.
4. List all dimensional inputs. If an obstruction concerns only the
   compatibility pattern, identify the additional mechanical calibration
   needed to speak about action. Then test rescaling against A16.

The first deliverable is a short premise-to-use table and explicit example
or counterexample, followed by one sequential librarian audit for any result.
Positivity, universality and an empirical numerical value remain distinct
selection obligations. No numerical verification scripts.

## What the sources contribute

Vasubandhu and Ibn Sina motivate explicit interface information. Liu Hui
motivates accounting for the remainder of a spatial decomposition. The
Mohists motivate separating a body from its apparatus-dependent effect.
Nagarjuna motivates tracking what a local predicate borrows from a relation.
These are constructions to think with, not proposed axioms attributed to the
authors.

R12's completed comparison of constituent and whole-body records gives an
exact information-loss benchmark. K01 supplies the targeted Q01 compatibility
test when that premise is selected in STATE. Its outcome should decide whether
the excluded classical model is excluded by an independently motivated rule;
temporal record formation can then be compared with the existing apparatus
counterexamples. An additional context or apparatus variant needs that same
decision link before becoming a successor task.
