## Reusable construction

Start from Newton's polygon, distinguish swept sectors from arc–chord error,
convert the error to action in the perpendicular constant-force example, and
ask which physical refinement condition gives a surviving resolution scale.
The ratio of orbital action to a proposed fixed action scale is a useful
dimensionless parameter. The receding-centre comparison can make the relation
between projectile geometry and central swept area precise.

The [area audit](../notes/receding-centre-area-audit.md) checks the finite-centre
limit and keeps matched-endpoint action differences separate from comparisons
ending at different positions. A03 returns these calculations to the continuum
limit of the cut points.

## Inferences to repair before reuse

1. A resolution threshold is an additional physical premise. Replacing a
   statement about mathematical limits by operational indistinguishability
   requires a defined observation protocol, not a change to real-number limits.
2. The proposed local formula uses force perpendicular to initial velocity.
   At a generic central-orbit point the force also has a tangential component;
   the configuration-area term uses the perpendicular component.
3. A minimum cell size $J\Delta\phi\ge h$ supplies a bound on the number of
   distinguishable steps. Exact tiling by cells of area $h$ is a further
   postulate. The essay uses this extra postulate to obtain $J=N\hbar$.
4. Whole-orbit action and uniformly advancing mean anomaly apply directly to
   the Kepler specialization. Generic bound central motion requires radial
   and angular actions and can have incommensurate frequencies.
5. High action relative to fixed $h$ is a useful physical correspondence
   regime. Holding $h$, $J=N\hbar$ and $J$ fixed while taking $N\to\infty$
   is inconsistent. A fixed classical action instead requires an appropriate
   joint formal limit, or one must compare a changing family of large-action
   orbits in scaled variables.
6. A derivative of the energy with respect to level label is different from
   an exact adjacent-level difference. The frequency argument needs that
   distinction. Short-step parabolic expansions also require large enough
   $N$ and cannot determine the one-vertex ground-state structure.
7. Keep reduced actions $I=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint p\,dq$ separate from unreduced
   integrals. EBK offsets, angular-momentum spectra and the interpretation of
   quantum states require their own established quantum/semi-classical premises.

These are draft audit questions and corrections, awaiting B11 review. The
conditional threshold branch remains useful beside the main programme of
deriving positive action from independently classical premises.
