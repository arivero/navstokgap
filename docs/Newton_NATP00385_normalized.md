## Representation and access caveats

The Newton Project describes this view as normalized: abbreviations are
expanded, editorial corrections are applied, additions are silently included,
and deleted text is hidden. The page reports that switching to the diplomatic
view exposes 7,123 deletions and 4,904 additions and removes 1,744 editorial
regularizations. It therefore cannot establish that words absent from this view
were never drafted.

The TEI header licenses the electronic text under CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 and labels its
availability “restricted.” This local snapshot is retained for source checking;
redistribution has not been cleared. The Newton Project supplies a transcription,
not a facsimile of the manuscript leaves.

The catalogue's “16 ff.” conflicts on its face with both the shelfmark span
ff. 594r-619v and the electronic text's many internal leaf/page labels. No
concordance from labels such as `<83r>` to the CUL shelf foliation was found in
the downloaded representations. Claims should cite the stable paragraph/line
anchors and describe the displayed labels as electronic-text labels, not silently
convert them into shelf folios.

## Extraction and reading coverage

The HTML was downloaded directly with `curl -L`. `lynx -dump -nolist -width=240`
produced a search aid of about 88,805 whitespace-delimited words; MathML and
editorial layout are not reliable in that dump. Paragraph IDs (`#par...`), line
IDs (`#l...`), and page IDs in the HTML remain the citation authority.

This was **not** a full read of the approximately 113,053-word e-text. A focused
search and selected-passage read covered moments, fluxions, nascent/evanescent or
infinitely small quantities, analytical versus geometrical demonstration,
Principia composition/publication, 1684-1687, discouragement, doubt, delay, and
related spelling variants. Exact selected coverage and findings are recorded in
[`notes/newton-NATP00385-audit.md`](../notes/newton-NATP00385-audit.md).
