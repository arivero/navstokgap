## Source Digest

The abstract states that a probability is the absolute square of a sum over alternatives/paths and that the contribution from a single path is postulated to be an exponential whose imaginary phase is the classical action in units of \hbar. The summed contribution is shown to satisfy Schrödinger’s equation.
