## Source Digest

The opening defines statistical distance between quantum preparations through statistical fluctuations in measurements intended to distinguish them. In the photon-polarization example, a finite number $n$ of photons gives sampling uncertainty and hence finite-trial indistinguishability. The paper’s headline result identifies this operational distance with Hilbert-space geometry for the quantum setting.

## Reading coverage and limits

Read: six-page archive source through the introduction and finite-$n$ setup (pp. 357–358). Later derivations were not proof-audited. The source assumes quantum preparations and does not map trajectory areas/action differences to state distance or derive $\hbar$/action gaps.
