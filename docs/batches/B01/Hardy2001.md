## Source Digest

Hardy presents a five-axiom reconstruction of quantum theory in a generalized state-space/probabilistic setting. The abstract and introduction say that the first four axioms are compatible with quantum and classical probability, while the continuity requirement in Axiom 5 excludes the classical alternative. This is useful for the project’s Branch B axiom-dependency audit.
