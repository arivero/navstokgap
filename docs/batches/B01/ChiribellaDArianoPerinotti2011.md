## Source Digest

The paper gives an operational-probabilistic reconstruction. Five principles (causality, perfect distinguishability, ideal compression, local distinguishability, pure conditioning) define a broad class, and purification singles out quantum theory within that class. The abstract also says mixed-state structure is derived without assuming the Hilbert-space framework.
