## Source Digest

The paper studies optimal binary discrimination of two quantum states with asymptotically many copies and identifies the quantum Chernoff error exponent. The abstract notes reduction to the classical Chernoff bound for commuting states and emphasizes operational meaning as a symmetric state-distance measure.
