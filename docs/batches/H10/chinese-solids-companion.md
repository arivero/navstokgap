# Liu Hui: a solid dissected before anything moves

The received commentary on *Nine Chapters*, juan 5, supplies a static
shrinking-remainder argument for the volume ratio of a yangma and a bienao.
This extends H08's circle-cutting source to solids.

## Selected text and working reading

Page 18 includes:

> 陽馬居二鼈臑居一

Working gloss: the yangma takes two shares and the bienao one. The surrounding
argument relates their dissection to the cube and discusses unequal dimensions.
Page 19 continues:

> 置餘廣袤高之數各半之則四分之三又可知也
> 半之彌少其餘彌細至細曰微微則無形由是言之安取餘哉

Working paraphrase: halve the remaining width, length and height; a further
three quarters can be accounted for. Repeated halving makes the remainder
finer, leading to the language of the subtle and the formless, and the question
of what remainder to retain. Punctuation and this English paraphrase are ours.
Read the complete neighbouring argument and its diagram before assigning an
exact modern recurrence to every named piece.

## Construction to borrow

Start with a static solid, a partition rule, an additive volume and a residual
estimate. Then ask which premise licenses passage from finite dissections to
the claimed ratio. This is a spatial precursor to the repository's cut-limit
question: the limiting remainder is already an issue before time enters.
The text's very-fine remainder belongs to an exhaustion argument, rather than
an asserted smallest positive physical volume. H11 should collate pages 18–19
against images before a manuscript uses the precise Chinese wording.
