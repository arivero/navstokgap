## Selected reading

[Chapter 3, B17](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-08797-4_3),
printed p. 132 (PDF page 142), reads: “A shadow does not shift about. The
explanation lies with being re-cast.” The canon's 徙 is emended from received
從 following Graham 1978, p. 82. The editors distinguish an extinguished and
re-cast optical effect from the transported object or light source.

B25a, printed p. 147 (PDF 157), contrasts a loaded wooden beam with a rope
sagging under its own weight, using rigidity. B25b, pp. 148–149 (PDF 158–159),
distinguishes weight from its effectiveness after a fulcrum is shifted. Its
canon is defective/restored; the commentary's apparatus reconstruction belongs
to the editors. This gives a static configuration-and-response source for I007.
