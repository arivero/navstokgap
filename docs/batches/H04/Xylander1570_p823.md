## Selected transcription and translation

At the bottom of the page, beginning *Porro autem vide quomodo occurrerit
Democrito*, the text presents the cone cut parallel to its base. The response
reads, with long-s normalised and line breaks joined:

> superficies ait neque aequales fieri neque inaequales, sed corpora inaequalia

Modern English translation: he says that the surfaces become neither equal nor
unequal, but the bodies unequal.

The running title is *Adversus Stoicos*. The modern reference is *De communibus
notitiis* §39, 1079E–1080A. The Latin witness establishes printed availability;
author-specific reception belongs to the separate H05 task.
