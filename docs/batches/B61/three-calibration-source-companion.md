## Claim audit

**C116 (global inverse after three calibrations): method-supported, model-derived.**
The sources justify the proof pattern: establish smooth dependence, construct
an inverse apparatus chart by contraction, and integrate a uniform derivative
margin over a convex receiver segment. R30 must itself prove that the selected
positive pulses make (L) invertible, that the analytic observability argument
applies to the stated receiver and pulse class, and that (6) holds uniformly on
the enlarged chart. Pointwise rank or a local inverse at the calibrated centre
does not imply the claimed global result. The exact calibration (q_1=c,
q_2=q_3=0), exact apparatus energy, fixed (c nonzero), and strict cutoff/existence
margins are model premises.

**C117 (precision closure): method-supported, model-derived.** Given C116 and
the stated chart derivative bounds, the estimator and the
(O((\delta/\lambda)^2)) canonical product follow by triangle inequalities and
the inverse stability estimate. The bound is in fixed component units until
the explicit (L_*P_*) conversion; it is not a sharp minimax result and does
not select a universal positive action parameter. A weak-coupling limit requires
\(\delta/\lambda\to0\); fixed positive coupling permits \(\delta\to0\).
