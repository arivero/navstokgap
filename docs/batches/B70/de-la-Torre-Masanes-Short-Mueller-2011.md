# Source companion: local quantum structure and reversibility

Gonzalo de la Torre, Lluis Masanes, Anthony J. Short and Markus P. Müller,
*Deriving quantum theory from its local structure and reversibility*,
[arXiv:1110.5482v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5482v1), submitted 25 October
2011; PDF prints 10 September 2018. Local original:
[PDF](de-la-Torre-Masanes-Short-Mueller-2011.pdf).

The [B70 digest](interaction-source-companion.md) records selected pp. 1–4
passages, theorem hypotheses, coordinator corrections and proof-audit limits.
The source supplies the reversible interaction dependency for Q01's C125 model;
it supplies no mechanical action parameter. This companion is a digest route,
not a full transcription or full proof review. Original PDF is preserved;
its SHA-256 is in docs/SHA256SUMS.
