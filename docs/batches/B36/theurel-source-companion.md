## Findings

The source's apparatus Hamiltonian is joint system plus apparatus plus
interaction. A conjugate pair `(Q,P)` is selected, with impulsive interaction
`alpha delta(t-t0) A Q`; the probe `Q` shifts the system along the Hamiltonian
flow of `A`, while the pointer records `P`. A trap prepares the apparatus near a
ground state; after thermalization at `T=1/(beta k_B)` it is isolated. Harmonic
normal form gives a selected mode frequency `Omega`; the ready-state Gaussian
has `Var(Q)=1/(beta Omega^2)` and `Var(P)=1/beta` in the unit-mass normal form
of (9)–(11). These correct the initial worker's two variance transcriptions.
The pointer is stable after
trap release by the involution assumption with the apparatus own Hamiltonian.

The source's `k=alpha^2 beta/8` has units `[A]^-2`;
`qbar=2/(beta Omega)=2 k_B T/Omega` has units of action. The imprecision
and disturbance satisfy `epsilon_A eta_A=qbar/2`, with
`epsilon_A=(alpha^2 beta)^(-1/2)` and `eta_A=(2k qbar^2)^(1/2)`. This is an
apparatus-specific scale. `eta_A` is the standard deviation of the random
Hamiltonian-flow parameter generated by A, with units action/[A]. It is not
generally the rms change of a chosen second observable. The author presents cooling/tightening as making it
smaller and relates the unattainable zero-quality limit to the third law. Thus
temperature unattainability is not a uniform positive lower bound over a model
class; it only makes each finite-temperature preparation have positive quality.

## Boundary and R06 use

The paper assumes an impulsive delta interaction and does not specify fixed total
apparatus mass, bounded force/coupling, finite pulse duration, an autonomous
clock, or a physical record-readout cost. Its thermal bath is a preparation
device, not a finite mechanical apparatus. Consequently the positive product is
an established conditional result, while any R06 finite-duration resource floor
is a project inference requiring new premises. The recommended next model is a
bounded-duration autonomous coupling with explicit clock and record, fixed mass
and coupling/force bounds, and a stated preparation constraint; prove or refute
that these premises bound `qbar` away from zero. Page 5 footnote 5 explicitly
uses a pulse of height proportional to inverse duration and neglects system
and apparatus drift in that limit. This is the concrete step for R06 to replace.

The selected passages supply positive trapped quadratic normal form, assumed
thermalization, a conserved pointer after release and ideal access to its value.
They do not derive preparation, switching or record-reading costs. Canonical
Gaussian tails also prevent this preparation from satisfying a hard speed
ceiling for every sample without a model change.
