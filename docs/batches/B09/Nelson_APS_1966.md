## Source digest

The abstract states the hypothesis that every particle of mass `m` undergoes
frictionless Brownian motion with diffusion coefficient `hbar/(2m)`, and that
the hypothesis leads to the Schrodinger equation. Thus Nelson supplies an
explicit mass-weighted diffusion coefficient as a premise. It does not supply
the finite-speed reversible Markov model, its plateau, or a derivation of that
coefficient from classical premises.
