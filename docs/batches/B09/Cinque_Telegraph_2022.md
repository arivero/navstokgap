## Source digest

The paper defines a two-velocity integrated motion driven by alternating
Poisson-type reversals, identifies the position/velocity pair as Markov, and
states the governing telegraph PDE (1.3). In the symmetric case it reduces to
the usual telegraph equation. The introduction also states generalized Kac
conditions under which the finite-velocity process converges to Brownian motion.
It does not state B09's mass-weighted variance observable or its iterated
second-moment limits.
