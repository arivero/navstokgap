## Source digest

Pavliotis writes the diffusion tensor as an integrated velocity correlation and,
for an ergodic Markov process with centered velocity, as a Poisson-equation
quadratic form. Proposition 2.1 gives `-L phi = V` and (2.3)--(2.5); pp. 5--6
derive the semigroup representation and nonnegativity. Reversibility gives a
symmetric tensor, but the source does not specialize to the finite-state,
strictly-positive spectral and finite-duration monotonicity theorem used in B09.
