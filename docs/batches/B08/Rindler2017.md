## Result used

Rindler distinguishes weak from strong convergence and identifies oscillations and concentrations as the phenomena retained by weak Sobolev limits. His elementary alternating-value sequence motivates Young measures. This supports the mechanism behind the draft's oscillatory velocities, but not its particular sine path or exact kinetic-action defect.

## Selected coverage

- Printed p. 16: the end of the direct-method discussion says that oscillations and concentrations distinguish weakly from strongly convergent Sobolev sequences.
- Printed pp. 56--57, section 3.3: motivation for Young measures and the sequence alternating between values (a,b) with volume fractions (\theta,1-\theta); nonlinear observables retain the mixture (\theta\alpha+(1-\theta)\beta).

Printed p. 57 has an apparent notation typo: it writes
(\nu_x=\theta\delta_\alpha+(1-\theta)\delta_\beta), although (\nu_x) is the measure on the input values and the preceding definitions require
(\nu_x=\theta\delta_a+(1-\theta)\delta_b). The displayed integral against (f(x,v)) and the theorem immediately below confirm that interpretation.
