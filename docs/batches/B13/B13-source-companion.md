## Barkai

> Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0303255, version v1
> Metadata: Eli Barkai, “Stable Equilibrium Based on Levy Statistics: A Linear Boltzmann Equation Approach,” 2003.
> Local original: `.build/b10/mai_2003_stable_equilibrium.pdf`
> Extraction: `pdftotext -layout`; p. 4 rendered and visually checked.

Selected pages are pp. 1, 3--5. Page 1 supplies abstract/model scope; p. 3
introduces the tracer/bath collision model and elastic premise; p. 4 contains
Eqs. (3)--(8), including the mass-ratio coefficients, uniform
rate, and master equation; p. 5 contains Eqs. (9)--(12), the Poisson collision
count interpretation. Only the collision premise/map is imported for C030.
The source assumes a bath and molecular-chaos setting and does not state the
equal-mass midpoint path, bounded-speed support theorem, or semigroup result.
Equation (2), the collision map, begins on p. 3. The equal-mass specialization
uses (2)–(3), not the divided density formula (5), which excludes that case.

## Cinque

> Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01904, version v1
> Metadata: Fabrizio Cinque, “A Note on the Conditional Probabilities of the Telegraph Process,” 2022.
> Local original: `.build/b09/Cinque2022.pdf`
> Extraction: `pdftotext -raw`; p. 2 rendered and visually checked.

Selected pages are pp. 1--2. Page 1 defines the two-velocity process and its
integrated position (Eqs. 1.1--1.2); p. 2 states finite-velocity endpoint atoms,
the telegraph equation (1.3), finite-velocity interpretation, and that the
position/velocity pair is Markov while position alone is non-Markov. It supports
the memory-bearing comparison only; it does not prove C032.
