## Claim-by-claim audit

**C106: exact complete-record compensator.** This is a model-derived
consequence conditional on the displayed free shear, compact smooth-flow
domain, and uniform derivative estimates. The inherited contraction passage
supports uniqueness once `T_w` maps the stated ball into itself with constant
at most one half. The existence of that ball, the bound proportional to
`lambda ||w-z_*||`, and the identification of the physical ten-record map
with `F_lambda` are R25 assumptions/derivations. The source does not establish
exact compensation or any rank statement for this apparatus.

**C107: full-shell minimax saturation.** This follows from C106 plus the
written shell geometry: when the weak-coupling inequality covers the compact
shell, one exact record is compatible with every shell point, while the
projection is the stated ellipse and the constant estimator attains the
coordinate extrema. The inherited sources provide no minimax canonical-risk,
projected-area, or full-shell saturation theorem for this model. The result
also remains conditional on the fixed positive receiver energy, bounded shell,
fixed preparation width, and explicit joint-limit ratio condition. It does not
select a universal positive action scale.
