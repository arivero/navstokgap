Publisher HTML: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0150171 (published 26 February 2016; CC BY). Equations are image placeholders in the HTML; no raw PDF was downloaded.

The source establishes the general precedent: Koopman pullback is an infinite-dimensional linear operator on observables; an invariant finite-dimensional observable subspace permits restriction to a finite-dimensional linear operator; exact state-advancing spaces are restrictive; exact invariant spaces must be distinguished from approximate truncations. It does not treat the two-sphere Hamiltonian in C127, the Vandermonde orbit, or the all-Borel-preparation expectation lemma.
