## Source digest

The transcript explains that single-valued periodic wavefunctions require (kL=2\pi n), hence (p=2\pi\hbar n/L), and gives (E_n=p_n^2/(2m)), including the (n=\pm1) degeneracy and first excitation (2\pi^2\hbar^2/(mL^2)). It supports M03's ring spectrum. The self-adjoint domain, interval spectrum, line spectrum, and (L\to\infty) limit are supplied by Teschl plus M03's Fourier derivation.
