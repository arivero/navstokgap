# Pitman and Yor (2018): bounded B12 source route

**Source.** Jim Pitman and Marc Yor, *A guide to Brownian motion and related
stochastic processes*, arXiv:1802.09679v1 (2018),
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09679>. The stable local PDF is the B06 cache
`.build/cone-refinement/PitmanYor2018.pdf`; its checksum and rights note are
recorded in `docs/batches/B06/PitmanYor2018.md`.

**Selected coverage (six printed pages maximum).** The B12 audit uses the
already verified passages on printed pp. 6, 10, 12–13: equations (4)–(5) for
the centered Brownian covariance, §3.4 for the endpoint bridge construction,
and equations (20)–(25) for Chapman–Kolmogorov and finite-dimensional Markov
products. These passages were visually checked in the B06 route; the PDF text
page offset is not a printed-page citation.

**Use and limit.** The source establishes the Gaussian bridge covariance and
the product/restriction framework. Replacing Brownian variance by
\(\kappa/m\) is a direct scaling. It does not establish the project’s action
interpretation, a positive universal \(\kappa\), the fixed-\(\kappa\) action
divergence, or the inserted-cut intervention law; those are derived in the
draft from finite-dimensional Gaussian algebra.

**Existing Newton route.** Newton’s projectile Scholium and Lemmas X–XI are
covered by the existing constant-force companion
`notes/principia-constant-force-action.md` (Motte/Chittenden 1846, printed
pp. 89, 99–101, with the surrounding Section I passages through pp. 103–106).
That historical source supports the parabola and lens ratios, not the modern
arbitrary-partition action formula.
