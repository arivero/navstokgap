## Representation and access caveats

The Newton Project calls this a diplomatic transcription with minimal editorial
intervention. It renders deletions and additions and leaves abbreviations
unexpanded. The page reports 7,123 deletions, 4,904 additions, and 1,744
regularizations suppressed in the normalized view. CSS classes and tooltip text
carry much of that revision information, so a generic text-only dump loses
important semantics. The TEI XML is preferable when the status of a particular
word or phrase matters.

The TEI header licenses the electronic text under CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 and marks the
text “restricted.” The snapshot is preserved locally for research verification;
redistribution has not been cleared. This is not a manuscript facsimile.

## Extraction and reading coverage

The HTML was retrieved directly with `curl -L`. A `lynx` dump was used only as a
search aid; it contains visible revision sigla but does not reliably preserve
nested deletions, additions, overwriting, MathML, or lineation.

Diplomatic/TEI spot checks were made for the principal passages used in the audit,
especially `#par489-#par490` (moments and first/last ratios), `#par520-#par522`
(analysis/synthesis), `#par686` (discouragement and earlier publication), and
`#par701` (avoiding a priority controversy). This was not a complete diplomatic
read. The material changes and ambiguous construction in `#par686` are recorded
in [`notes/newton-NATP00385-audit.md`](../notes/newton-NATP00385-audit.md).
