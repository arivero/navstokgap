## Source digest

This author review describes rigorous work in Euclidean dimensions two and three at finite volume. Its results concern renormalised stochastic gauge dynamics and processes on gauge orbits. It supports a bridge through parabolic analysis, not a solution in four dimensions.

## Source-facing notes

Read the introduction, displayed stochastic dynamics, dimension-dependent theorem statements and open questions. Distinguish local construction, global existence and invariant-measure identification: none should be inferred solely from the phrase “Markov process.”

Review scope: selected statements, not all proofs or the underlying papers. The 3D Yang–Mills–Higgs paper mentioned in the initial README is discussed through this review for this milestone.
