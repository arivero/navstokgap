The free operator is the Fourier multiplier with spectrum $[0,\infty)$ and
domain $H^2$. Its propagator has the oscillatory Gaussian kernel. The oscillator
section constructs the one-dimensional Hermite ladder before stating a
three-dimensional spectrum in Theorem 8.5. M03 uses the one-dimensional
construction and restores $m,\hbar$ from the source convention $m=1/2,\hbar=1$.
Its full diagonal domain and completeness proof are written out in the manuscript.
