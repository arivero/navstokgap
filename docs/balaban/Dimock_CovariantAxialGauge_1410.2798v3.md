# Covariant Axial Gauge

The paper develops axial gauge fixing compatible with lattice symmetries and block averaging, in an Abelian setting.

Interesting points from the introduction:

- A fixed tree gives an effective way to impose axial gauge but can spoil lattice covariance. The introduction describes Balaban’s use of averaging over trees to recover covariance.
- Section 2 starts with a finite lattice cube. Section 3 develops the toroidal multiscale setting needed when the rescaled unit lattice is large.
- The paper treats delta-function gauge fixing and explains its relation to the earlier exponential-gauge formulation. Results from one convention should not be transferred without checking that change.

Project connection (inference): a proposed blocking map has to carry an actual gauge-fixing and symmetry argument. Abelian formulas may provide controls for our gauge refinements, but this paper does not establish their non-Abelian analogue or a mass gap.
