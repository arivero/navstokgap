# The Algebra of Block Spin Renormalization Group Transformations

This paper isolates algebraic identities for block-spin transformations in a programme on weakly interacting bosons.

Interesting points from the introduction and Proposition 4:

- Background fields and critical fields have different roles: one represents previous eliminations; the other solves the next stationary-point problem.
- Proposition 4(c) gives the composition rule: composing the old background field with the new critical field produces a next-scale background field, under the stated domain and stationary-point hypotheses.
- Proposition 4(b) identifies the corresponding effective actions. Part (d) retains a fluctuation integral and an additional domain-complement contribution.

Project connection (inference): this provides a useful algebraic comparator for our point/cell insertion laws. Composition of stationary fields is one layer; controlling the fluctuation integral and the excluded domain is additional work. The paper’s bosonic setting does not establish gauge-theory convergence or action-scale necessity.
