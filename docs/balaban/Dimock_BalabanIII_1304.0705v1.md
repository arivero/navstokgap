# The Renormalization Group According to Balaban - III. Convergence

The third exposition completes convergence and a stability bound for the finite-volume three-dimensional scalar model. Section 3.6 turns connected-polymer estimates into a bound on the normalized partition function.

Interesting points from the selected passages:

- The introduction recalls a density representation that retains region histories, normalization and boundary terms after every RG step.
- Lemmas 16–17 in section 3.6 combine tree-length decay with small activity factors, controlling the sum over connected configurations.
- Theorem 2, equations (221)–(222), writes the partition function relative to its Gaussian counterpart as the exponential of a convergent connected-polymer sum, for sufficiently small coupling and the stated counterterms.
- Corollary 1, equation (225), bounds that ratio above and below exponentially in physical volume, with estimates uniform in the ultraviolet cutoff.

Project connection (inference): convergence of the expansions and stability of the partition function are concrete targets for a refinement construction. They must be distinguished from convergence of all observables, reconstruction of a continuum theory, and a positive physical spectral gap.
