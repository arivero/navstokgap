# Nonperturbative renormalization of scalar QED in d=3

The paper studies renormalization of scalar QED on a three-dimensional torus in a bounded-field approximation. The opening explicitly limits the task to the ultraviolet problem and excludes the infinite-volume limit.

Interesting points from section 1.1:

- Counterterms are selected by treating the RG flow as a discrete dynamical system, with prescribed terminal vacuum energy and scalar mass.
- This formulation avoids expanding in the coupling and drawing Feynman diagrams for its renormalization procedure.
- The bounded-field analysis is distinct from the large-field completion needed for an unrestricted stability result.
- The introduction explains why estimates of the partition function alone are awkward to extend to correlation functions, and why developing the full RG flow helps.

Project connection (inference): a terminal-condition formulation of the coupling flow is a useful alternative to chasing a single perturbative threshold. This is a superrenormalizable Abelian model with matter; it does not supply our four-dimensional SU(3) continuum construction.
