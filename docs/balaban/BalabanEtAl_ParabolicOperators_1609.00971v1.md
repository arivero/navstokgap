# Operators for Parabolic Block Spin Transformations

The paper supplies operator estimates for a small-field parabolic RG programme for weakly interacting bosons. It concerns the fluctuation covariance, Green functions and linear parts of the background/critical fields.

Interesting points from the selected passages:

- The introduction uses blocks of temporal length L squared and spatial length L. This anisotropic scaling belongs to the bosonic parabolic problem.
- The contents separate block-spin operators, differential operators, covariance, Green functions and the degree-one critical field. The analytic ingredients have individual obligations.
- Proposition 4.4 uses spectral separation and analytic functional calculus to bound functions of the covariance-related operator in a weighted norm. This includes more than an estimate of the inverse alone.

Project connection (inference): estimates for covariance functions and their localization are useful targets when replacing correlated fluctuations by integration variables. Their constants and anisotropic scaling cannot be imported directly into isotropic four-dimensional Yang–Mills blocking.
