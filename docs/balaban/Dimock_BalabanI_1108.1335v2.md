# The Renormalization Group According to Balaban - I. Small fields

The exposition treats scalar phi-four theory on a finite three-dimensional torus. Its small-field step preserves a controlled class of effective densities (Theorem 14), under sufficiently large block parameters and sufficiently small running coupling.

Interesting points from the selected passages:

- Section 2.1, equations (18)–(23), inserts a normalized Gaussian block-averaging kernel and rescales. The integral of the density is preserved. This is a change of description with an exact normalization identity.
- Section 2.4, equations (79)–(85), constructs the Green function from local Neumann inverses and a convergent random-walk expansion. Local decay and locality of dependence are ingredients for later bounds.
- Section 4.5 explains why an interaction local in a correlated fluctuation field need not be local in the independent Gaussian variables. Weakening parameters and localization repair this before the cluster expansion.
- Theorem 14 separates vacuum energy, mass, quartic coupling and polymer remainder. All four participate in the induction.

Project connection (inference): the object to propagate in our halving programme is a structured effective density with controlled remainders. An identity for coarse links alone cannot replace that control. The decay here is not a physical Yang–Mills spectral gap.
