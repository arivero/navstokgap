# On the section of a cone

Rivero retains the separation data of two approaching faces and compares
successive cylinder approximations at a common scale.

- Author: Alejandro Rivero; arXiv:math/9904021v1, submitted 5 April 1999.
- [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9904021v1),
  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9904021v1).
- Coverage: all five pages read on 2026-09-06; pp. 2–5 revisited 2026-09-07.
  PDF text extraction; bibliographic year follows the arXiv submission record.
- Anchors: p. 2, thinning cutter/two planes; p. 3, surviving differential
  structure; p. 4, volume-preserving pair averaging; p. 5, time interpretation
  and tangent-groupoid suggestion.
- Attribution: the account develops a modern mathematical interpretation of
  Plutarch's report, informed by Agustín García Calvo's presentation.
- Rights: arXiv link; any downloaded research copy stays in ignored `.build/`.
