## Source digest

Lemma I treats limits. Lemmas II–III treat inscribed and circumscribed sums. Lemma X concerns the initial quadratic displacement generated by force. Lemma XI and Corollaries 4–5 give cubic tangent-triangle/segment scaling. The concluding Scholium distinguishes limits from quantities of a smallest determinate magnitude.

## Source-facing notes

The English excerpt is an independent digital transcription of Motte, not a facsimile of 1687. Lemma XI's finite nonzero curvature qualification matters. Read with the 1846 opening material for the laws and Proposition I, which are outside this excerpt. No claim about the complete historiography of these passages is supported by this reading.
