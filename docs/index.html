What survives refinement?

Two proof goals guide this repository: derive a positive universal action scale from independently justified physical premises in Newton’s Galileo comparison, and construct pure SU(3) Yang–Mills in four dimensions with a positive finite physical mass gap.

Exact insertion laws, conditional record bounds and controlled lattice estimates are in hand. Both main goals remain open. The pion is a symmetry benchmark for the comparison.

Strong results to start with

Newton’s action decomposes under every cut

The cut measure gives exact additive refinement costs. Constant-force and harmonic curves can be constructed under the stated laws for arbitrary shrinking partitions: construction and limit. Consistency alone leaves zero action admissible.

A record costs disturbance

General quantum instruments obey a sagitta/impulse disturbance bound with supplied ℏ and explicit comparison-state resources. The sharp Gaussian floor has narrower mark and preparation-invariance hypotheses; prepared packets show why a universal cell-action floor needs more.

Series closes; parallel carries the generated action

Directional gauge halving factors exactly. SU(2) midpoint traces and general compact-group cuts are exact; interacting mid-plane comparison remains a separate estimate.

Connected bad sets and exact dressing in a weak strip

All-order marked estimates (116) and dressed activities (112) are proved in their retained-barrier weak-recoupling domain. Section 37 gives a quadratic moving-source test for part of (113). These results do not yet give the full nonlinear group integral or stable physical iteration.

An explicit strong-coupling finish line

Volume-uniform lattice gaps and robust target bounds are established in their stated regimes. The Wilson note separates its leading-activity 176 estimate from the conservative all-representation activity criterion; the KS bound uses a different Hamiltonian and threshold. Controlled arrival from the continuum scaling trajectory remains open.

Current open fronts

  • Newton: a physical history rule that prepares and carries coherence memory through separation and reunion, survives complete records, and independently excludes the zero branch. Current packet/stability test uses supplied quantum free evolution as its reference.
  • Gauge local step: complex nonlinear centered bounds and decaying source dependence of the moving saddle, with the barrier retained. Current quadratic input.
  • Four dimensions and spectrum: depth-uniform generated-action matching, stable iteration, and the renormalized small-volume bridge. Continuum axioms, nontriviality, volume uniformity and physical spectral weight remain explicit.

Decisive corrections to retrieve

  • Local Agmon suppression: original route → global-only correction.
  • Finite crossover certification and monotonicity: earlier bridge → weak-side diagnosis.
  • Naive blocking, invertible flow and bare Feshbach UV control: scoped failures and surviving alternatives.
  • A prior covariance restriction is vulnerable to complete and nonlinear records: exact tests. Thermodynamic costs and phase consistency supply conditional trades or ratios, while positivity remains separate.
