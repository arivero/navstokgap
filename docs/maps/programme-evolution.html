From the September programme to the current graph

2026-10-01. PROGRAMME, STRATEGY, action target, and cut target are explicitly historical. AGENTS and STATE govern current work. This comparison changes no priorities.

The 16 September programme organized positive-action foundations around quantum exclusion/reconstruction, five action-selection obligations, finite apparatus and robust gap examples. The 17–23 September direction concentrated on the Newton paper and completed broad record/disturbance results. From 26 September the two proof goals were again active in a shared refinement frame, with the pion as orientation. By the current STATE, exact series/parallel insertion, retained-barrier clusters, generated action and complete-record physical mechanisms determine the live dependencies. This is a change of mathematical architecture, not just terminology.

The five gates remain a good logical checklist. They are a poor chapter sequence if repeated as inventories: current chapters should instead develop the mechanisms and show which gates each actually discharges. The early programme explains why the corpus contains apparatus, detector, collision, spin and source clusters that are no longer immediate prerequisites.

Preserve its distinctions between clocks, physical operators, preparation resources, universality and quantum identification. Keep its task tables, publication ordering and per-step bookkeeping as research history. Later central concepts absent or undeveloped there are the exact insertion spine, the series/parallel split, centre-sensitive cut fractions, normalized bad-set clusters, weak-strip dressing, generated-action depth control, complete-record nonlinear countertests and phase-memory stability. These determine the preferred book structure and current dependency map.
