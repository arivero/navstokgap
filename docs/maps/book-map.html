Book architecture by scientific question

This is a placement map for the eventual comparison. STATE owns research priorities; dependencies owns proof inputs and the catalog covers the corpus. Chapter placement confers neither proof status nor novelty. Both main goals remain open.

Part Question Primary treatments
Refinement What must an inserted variable preserve? Exact composition, classical construction, cut geometry
State What state information crosses a cut? Receiver memory, cusp histories, orientation descent
Records What does a retained record determine or disturb? Gaussian marks, quantum resources, apparatus calibration, force/minimax
Quantum construction Which classical data support a quantum family? Bounded lift, smooth products, groupoid states, walls
Selection Which additional premises select a floor, a unit or quantum structure? Units/orbits, radiation/record controls, Gaussian closure, meter countertest
Full controllers Can the stated restriction survive all accessible records? Complete recording, score field action, coherence
Gauge refinement Which generated interactions remain controlled? Atlas, series/parallel, mid-plane estimates, 4D composition
Spectrum Which spectral weight survives volume and cutoff removal? Lattice/continuum obligations, small volume, valley lifting, pion comparison
Sources What do the historical texts commit to? Planck working manuscript, cut provenance, source archive

The integrated Planck manuscript retains its source-based historical exposition; technical canonical notes hold the reusable proofs and their exact domains. Independent oscillator, spin, transport and regulator manuscripts retain their own constructions and source evidence; manuscript classifications identify their current or historical use. Failed routes belong beside the claim they restrict, with obstructions supplying retrieval.

Comments, corrections and questions are welcome as repository issues. Open one at https://github.com/arivero/navstokgap/issues, or go straight to a new issue. Corrections to a quoted source, a dating or a proof are especially wanted: every note names the premises it uses, so a disputed premise can be pointed at directly.
