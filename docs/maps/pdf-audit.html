action-gap-foundations Exact matched-endpoint constant-force chord excess F^2 T^3/(24m), its tangent-area relation, and the zero-infimum distinction between finite variations and their quadratic cost. The conditional finite-copy two-arm discrimination threshold and its collapse as the copy count grows when visibility is below one; its supplied quantum states, phase rule, and measurement model remain assumptions. The explicit separation of Jacobi stability, free-kernel short-time behavior, and relativistic speed-margin examples from any universal action-floor claim. Placement: technical appendix. action-scale-obstructions
classical-action-field Bounded-speed displacement variance gives a short-window coefficient at most mu^2 Delta and hence zero as the window shrinks. For a finite irreducible reversible velocity chain, the exact positive Green–Kubo plateau is 2m⟨v,(-Q)^{-1}v⟩, with spectral bounds; the two-speed case gives mu^2/lambda. The iterated rate/duration limits differ under the formal scaling u_lambda^2=a lambda, while a fixed speed ceiling prevents this scaling from supplying a physical universal constant. Placement: technical appendix. composition-universality
classical-spins-operational-closure C125: affine orientation effects give the Bloch ball and capacity two, while the specified classical composites have capacity 2^n and dimension 4^n but product-only pure states, so purification and the stated subspace requirement fail. C127: the reversible H=J u_z v_z interaction evolves identical retained first/cross moments into different product-test probabilities. C128: for all Borel preparations the interaction has no finite-dimensional exact observable closure, including nonlinear updates of finitely many expectation coordinates. Placement: teaching standalone. classical-orientation-closure
collision-action-relaxation The refreshed elastic bath has exact covariance plateau (m+M)s^2/nu and physical-time exponential relaxation; rescaling the refreshed velocity amplitude makes the plateau arbitrarily small. C050: the equal-mass Poisson hard-point gas gives an infinite-gas plateau mu/rho at collision rate rho u. C051: ordered counterpropagating lattice streams with the same m,u,rho and mean collision rate have periodic tagged motion and zero long-window response; the difference is the joint position–velocity preparation. Placement: technical appendix. ordered-beam-preparation
regulator-limits For arbitrary partitions, the normalized Gaussian bridge has exact determinant and covariance formulas and its polygonal paths converge uniformly under a common Brownian-bridge coupling as the mesh fluctuation scale vanishes. With (N−1)kappa tending to a positive constant, the paths converge to the straight path while the excess action converges in L2 to half that constant; the finite-dimensional action distribution still has values arbitrarily close to zero. The bounded-speed sine example, fixed-dimensional stationary-phase/delta-weight calculation, and two-regulator parameter map preserve the distinction between a residual limit and a hard finite-path floor. Placement: technical appendix. two-regulator-audit
research-programme A dated record of the programme map and research priorities as of 2026-09-16, including the provenance and then-current organization of action-selection, operational-closure, and mass-gap routes. Historical context for claims and publications assembled at that date; it contributes no independent theorem and explicitly labels itself superseded by AGENTS.md. Placement: history. programme evolution
same-collisions-different-transport An accessible derivation of the C050 Poisson-gas plateau and C051 ordered-stream vanishing response under matched mass, speed, density, and mean collision rate. The Palm residual-time distinction: the ordered preparation has a uniform residual wait even though its long-run collision rate is the same; the manuscript explains the full-period cancellation and includes teaching questions. Placement: teaching standalone. ordered-beam-preparation
time-refinement Exact Gaussian convolution under arbitrary insertion of time points leaves every positive action parameter free, with the deterministic zero-width member also allowed. The bridge mean and variance concentrate on the straight path as the scale vanishes, without selecting the scale; the free unitary Fourier multiplier has generator H_kappa=kappa2(-d2/dx^2)/(2m) on the stated domain. The analytic continuation from the positive Gaussian family to oscillatory unitary evolution is identified as a model choice, not a consequence of refinement. Placement: technical appendix. refinement-composition-and-limit
