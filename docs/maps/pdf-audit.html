action-gap-foundations orphan manuscript. This early synthesis preserves useful accepted calculations and their premise boundaries, but it predates the later action-scale, record, and Newton insertion work; its central status map is superseded by action-scale-obstructions, while the detailed propositions and quantitative two-arm calculation remain useful as an archival technical source. The quantum discrimination result is conditional and does not establish classical necessity. Canonical treatment.
arrow-not-sling historical research snapshot. Reads ancient arrow arguments as first-order motion questions, Newton’s sling as second-order deviation under inertia, and the firebrand circle as sampling/appearance. Scope: The corrected note explicitly retains Plutarch’s sling–moon witness; any claim that ancient philosophical sling arguments are absent is false. The force-rung analogy uses planck-gap-paper, not a historical transmission proof. Canonical treatment.
classical-action-field orphan manuscript. The later composition-universality note uses the C019–C020 plateau as input and proves a conditional common-coefficient result, but does not reproduce the finite-chain spectral derivation or the short-window and noncommuting-limit calculations. The manuscript therefore retains the detailed proof source for C018–C021; its physical universality question remains explicitly open, and the plateau is not a quantum phase constant. Canonical treatment.
classical-spins-operational-closure working manuscript. Independent article exposition of classical spin operational descent and finite-observable obstruction. The canonical orientation treatment integrates the subsequent generator/source qualifications; this earlier article is retained as a working manuscript. Canonical treatment.
collision-action-relaxation orphan manuscript. The composition-universality note summarizes the paired C050/C051 preparation countertest. It does not replace the full bath calculation or the paired construction, so this manuscript remains their technical source. The examples show preparation and rate dependence, not a universal action floor. Canonical treatment.
regulator-limits orphan manuscript. The Newton synthesis restates the distinct path, action, and scale limits, but the arbitrary-partition determinant, normalized Gaussian calculation, scalar stationary-phase limit, and parameter map remain detailed here. Its contribution is a regime-dependent finite defect, not a positive lower bound on all admissible paths. Canonical treatment.
research-programme historical research snapshot. This is a generated historical research source rather than an orphan result manuscript: its TeX identifies research/PROGRAMME.md as its maintained source, and the PDF labels its 2026-09-16 content historical and superseded by AGENTS.md. Preserve it for provenance only; its former priorities and process instructions must not be treated as live state, which is now carried by research/STATE.md and the governing repository instructions.
same-collisions-different-transport orphan manuscript. This manuscript adds no new claim beyond C050–C051 and is not the maintained proof source; it is a reader-oriented standalone exposition of the exact matched-parameter counterexample, with the maintained C051 proof linked through composition-universality. Retain it if the teaching/publication use is intended, since its comparison and Palm-clock explanation are easier to teach than the technical collision paper. Canonical treatment.
time-refinement orphan manuscript. The later refinement-composition-and-limit note incorporates the exact Gaussian insertion/composition law and places it inside the broader Newton and gauge refinement construction. This earlier paper remains a compact, detailed source for the free-kernel family, bridge concentration, and unitary generator; its central conclusion is that refinement alone does not select a positive action scale, and positivity is an additional premise. Canonical treatment.
