Source digest

Archimedes’ preface to Eratosthenes states that Democritus first asserted, without proof, that cone and pyramid are one third of cylinder and prism, and that Eudoxus proved it. Heath’s introductory note (pp. 8–11) quotes Plutarch’s cone dilemma in full and reads the phrase “made up of equal circles” as evidence that Democritus already treated a solid as a sum of parallel laminae, the idea the mechanical method then uses by balancing sections. For this project, this joins the Plutarch passage of H04 to the earliest ancient use of sections as a method of discovery, and it is the ancient counterpart of Cavalieri’s “all the lines”.
