# Eid et al. — potassium transmission through optical barriers

Roy Eid, Alfred Hammond, Lucas Lavoine and Thomas Bourdel measured the
transmitted and reflected fractions of a potassium-39 cloud accelerated
along a guide toward a repulsive laser barrier. For a fixed narrow
barrier the measured transmission falls over a broad range of laser
powers. The authors compare it with a quantum scattering calculation
and study optical aberrations by varying the projected mask.
[Published paper, *Physical Review A* **110**, 043316 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.110.043316)
and [authors' May 2024 version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14466v1).

## Reading coverage

The apparatus paragraphs preceding Figs. 1 and 2 specify the magnetic
acceleration, the projected pixel pitch, a cold launch velocity spread
inferred from expansion, a thermal fraction and an initial cloud size
estimated using the trap frequency and expansion energy. The low-speed
run uses one three-pixel mask. The plotted horizontal coordinate is
barrier height relative to full power. The acceleration and release
distance set the incident velocity. The side counts are obtained by
fluorescence imaging after separation. The authors discuss thermal
atoms that have yet to meet the barrier at the observation time.
[Apparatus and first transmission measurement](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14466v1#S0.F1)
(passages).
