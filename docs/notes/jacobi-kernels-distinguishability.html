The operational result assumes the specified quantum states, phase rule, equal priors and joint binary measurements on \(N\) independent copies. At fixed \(1/2<p<1\) its threshold tends to zero as \(N\) grows; at \(p=1\) it stays \(\pi\hbar\) for every finite \(N\).
