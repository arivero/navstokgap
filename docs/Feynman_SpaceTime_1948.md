## Source digest

Feynman presents a nonrelativistic quantum formulation using amplitudes associated
with paths and action-dependent phases. This is the historical primary reference
for the path-phase framework, not a derivation of Planck's constant from Newtonian
geometry. The repo's finite-dimensional kernel and discrimination proofs are
written out independently; no unread page is cited as proof of those calculations.
