# Umāsvāti, Tattvārthasūtra V: dharma and adharma as the media of motion and rest

> Source: [Sanskrit Wikisource, तत्त्वार्थसूत्रम्/पञ्चमोऽध्यायः](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/तत्त्वार्थसूत्रम्/पञ्चमोऽध्यायः), wikitext through the MediaWiki API with markup stripped; local verbatim excerpt [`.txt`](Tattvarthasutra_ch5_wikisource_excerpt.txt); retrieved 2026-09-18.

## What it supplies

**The only system in this collection with a medium whose sole office is to
make motion possible.** Sūtra 5.17:

> *गतिस्थित्युपग्रहौ धर्माधर्मयोरुपकारः* — the assisting of motion and of
> rest is the function of dharma and adharma.

The word is *upagraha*, assistance, and *upakāra*, function or service: these
substances do not push anything. They are the standing condition without
which motion and rest could not occur, which is a third option beside a cause
that acts and a void that offers no resistance. Sūtra 5.18 gives space the
distinct office of accommodation, *avagāha*, so that occupying room and being
able to move are separated into two substances. Sūtra 5.22 assigns duration
and activity to time. Where the Vaiśeṣika asks what *carries* motion from
step to step and answers with an impression, the Jain asks what *permits* it
at all and answers with a medium; the two questions are different, and the
collection now holds one source for each.

**A stopping rule stated as a count.** Sūtras 5.7 to 5.11 give the number of
*pradeśas*, space-points, of each substance, and 5.11 is a single word:
*नाणोः*, "not of the atom". The atom has no space-points, so it is not
spatially divisible even in principle. Sūtra 5.27, *भेदादणुः*, makes the atom
arise by division only, never by combination, which fixes the direction in
which the division series terminates.

## Coverage and limits

Reading level `passage` for the ten sūtras in the excerpt; `metadata` for the
rest of the work. No commentary is stored: neither Umāsvāti's own
*Svopajñabhāṣya* nor Pūjyapāda's *Sarvārthasiddhi*, and the latter is the
Digambara recension's witness and the place where the numbering divergence
would have to be settled. The claim that a Jain atom crosses the universe in
one *samaya*, which would give a minimum quantum of motion, is **not** in
this chapter and is not covered here.
