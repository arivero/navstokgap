## Source digest

The third Exercitatio answers Guldin's objections in the Centrobaryca, chiefly that indivisibles cannot compose a continuum and that comparing infinite aggregates is illegitimate, by restating that the method compares aggregates of lines or planes taken with a common regula and by supplying a second method that avoids the aggregates. For this project, this is the seventeenth-century record of the objection that a cut-refinement law changes the object, and of the reply that only ratios of aggregates are asserted.
