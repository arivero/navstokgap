## Source digest

Legge renders the debaters' theses, including “The shadow of a flying bird does not (itself) move” and “Swift as the arrowhead is, there is a time when it is neither flying nor at rest”, with his note that these are riddles or paradoxes. For this project, this is the pre-1901 English of the Chinese arrow beside the [Chinese text](Zhuangzi_33_Tianxia_zh_wikisource.md).
