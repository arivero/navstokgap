## Source digest

The preface argues that the physical astronomy of the book was known to the oldest philosophers: that celestial bodies gravitate mutually, are retained in their orbits by gravity, and that the law of gravity was understood, with Anaxagoras, Archelaus, Euripides and Pythagoras cited. The programmatic sentence begins “Ne cui vero Physica hic tradita, quasi novum aliquid et insolens in Astronomia videatur, eandem vetustissimis Philosophis notam … ostendam.”

For this project, this is the printed witness closest to Newton's unpublished Classical Scholia, the target of H02 and H03. Newton supplied Gregory with material for this preface, according to the scholarship listed in the [H02 seeds](../../references/batches/H02-seeds.md), which remains to be read at passage level. The preface therefore lets the historical track quote a pre-1701 printed text while the manuscript corpus stays inaccessible.
