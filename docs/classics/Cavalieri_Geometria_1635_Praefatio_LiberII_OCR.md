## Source digest

The preface presents the method; Book II defines “all the lines” of a plane figure, taken with a common regula, and “all the planes” of a solid, and states the fundamental theorem that figures are in the ratio of their aggregates of lines. For this project, this is the printed form of the position defended in the 1634 letter, and the source Guldin attacked.
