## Source digest

In the first letter Cavalieri defines “all the lines” of a plane figure as the parallels drawn through it and asks Galileo whether all the lines of one figure have a ratio to those of another, since they seem infinite. In the second he develops the comparison of all the lines of two figures and the objection that multiplying a figure multiplies its lines. These are the earliest statements of the method of indivisibles, sent to Galileo for judgement. For this project, they show the cut question posed as a question about ratios of aggregates of sections, before the treatise of 1635.
