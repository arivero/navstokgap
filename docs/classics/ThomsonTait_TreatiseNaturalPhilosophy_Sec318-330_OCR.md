## Source digest

Sections 318–325 present Hamilton's characteristic function, the law of varying action and its applications, and §§326–330 present Maupertuis's principle of least action. Section 327 states it as: of all the sets of paths along which a conservative system may be guided from one configuration to another with the sum of its potential and kinetic energies equal to a given constant, the one for which the action is least is such that the system, started with the proper velocities, moves along it unguided.

For this project, this is the standard nineteenth-century English textbook statement of the fixed-energy principle, placed beside [Hamilton](Hamilton_GeneralMethodDynamics_1834_Wilkins.md) and [Jacobi](Jacobi_VorlesungenDynamik_Lect6-7_1866_OCR.md); it fixes the action as twice the time integral of the kinetic energy and keeps the energy constraint explicit.
