## Source digest

Berkeley's objection is that fluxions and evanescent increments are “neither finite Quantities nor Quantities infinitely small, nor yet nothing”, hence “the Ghosts of departed Quantities” (§35). Sections 13–16 argue that correct results in the calculus arise from compensating errors, and the closing Queries ask whether the objects of the method are conceivable at all.

For this project, the tract is the canonical statement of the doubt about vanishing magnitudes that Newton's closing Scholium to Book I, Section I anticipates, and it frames hypothesis H002 about the delay of the *Principia*. The project's finite-speed crossover, which makes a positive coefficient possible only above a window $\Delta_*$, is a physical answer of a different kind from the logical repair Berkeley demanded.
