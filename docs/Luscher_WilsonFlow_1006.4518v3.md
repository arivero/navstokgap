## Source digest

The continuum flow equations (1.1)–(1.2) and their gauge-modified form (2.2) give a concrete auxiliary smoothing evolution for gauge fields.

## Source-facing notes

Only flow definitions and their interpretation are used here. A smoothed observable at positive flow time is not a construction of the underlying quantum measure. The paper's numerical findings and corrected numerical values are not used in this comparison.

Review scope: introductory equations and §2.1; not a general proof of continuum quantum existence.
