# Ordinary Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems

Gerald Teschl, AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics 140 (2012), ISBN
978-0-8218-8328-0. The author-hosted preliminary version has an April
2012 preface.

Sources: [author book page](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-ode/ode)
and [author PDF](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-ode/ode.pdf).
Retrieved 2026-10-27. Publisher metadata was checked on the author page.

## Reading record

Passages read using `pdftotext -layout`: section 2.7, printed pp.54--56,
Euler construction, compactness and the Peano theorem (Theorem 2.19);
Problem 2.25, printed p.58, the Osgood sufficient uniqueness condition
and its separation-of-variables hint. The book was not read in full.

The Newton continuity note uses the compactness/residual construction
for arbitrary vanishing meshes. Its conservative arrival integral and
logarithmic threshold are derived explicitly in the note. The general
first-order Osgood test and that sharper conservative calculation have
different hypotheses; failure of a sufficient test does not imply
nonuniqueness.
