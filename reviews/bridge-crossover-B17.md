# Review: bridge crossover (B17)

The C037 fixed-count midpoint calculation is sound on the stated telegraph bridge domain. The odd/even midpoint sectors produce the displayed factorial sum; the position Jacobian and Vandermonde identity give the normalized Beta(k+1,k) density for k>=1. The k=0 endpoint atom must remain separate and has mass 1/I0(z). The exact first/second moments, mixture ratio R, cubic onset, and unscaled-mean correction are consistent with the 29 exact checks and seven quadrature comparisons.

C037’s external status is derived consequence, not established prior art. Cinque pp. 3–4, Remark 2.1/(2.4) and Theorem 2.1/(2.6), support the alternating-sum occupation representation and conditional density. They do not state the return-bridge midpoint beta law or its action normalization. Bogachev–Ratanov concerns half-line occupation and supplies no bridge match. DLMF 10.32.1 supports only the Bessel integral representation.

C038 is also a valid derived necessary-bound argument: a common finite window, bounded speed, and masses tending to zero force a common nonnegative coefficient to vanish. The bound is necessary, not sufficient. The proof does not assert monotonicity of g, and no monotonicity is needed for either endpoint limit. Mass-dependent crossover windows remain compatible with a positive shared plateau.

No proof defect found. Keep endpoint conditioning/version conventions, m,u,lambda,T>0, and the separate H* and hbar identification assumption explicit.
