Since the effects contain u_x and u_y, w=u_x+i u_y lies in the complexification. The explicit flow gives U_t w=w exp(i Omega_A t v_z). Differentiating at z=0 through order m-1 gives a Vandermonde system in distinct t_j, with nonzero determinant when J,S_A>0. Thus arbitrarily many orbit elements are independent. Only displayed exponentials are differentiated, so no regularity of added observables is assumed.

Inclusion of 1 gives equal nonzero positive and negative masses; normalization yields two probability measures with equal retained expectations and different g-expectations. Hence an update of the finite expectation vector for every preparation forces each U_t f_k into the retained span, even when the proposed coordinate update is nonlinear and single-valued.
