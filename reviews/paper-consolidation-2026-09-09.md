# Consolidation of the short papers

A06 and A09a now form one maintained return-bridge manuscript. A10 and A11
likewise share one receiver manuscript. The first merge removes a duplicated
preparation, count-weight display and separate closing handoff while preserving
the path-law, beta-law, moment, cut and mass-window proofs.

## A01–A09 comparison

| Group | Shared material | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A06 + A09a | Same conditioned telegraph path, endpoint convention, count weights and midpoint variable | Merged in `telegraph-return-bridge`; full proofs retained, common setup stated once |
| A01 + A02 + A08 | Displacement-action definition, covariance/plateau framework and preparation-dependence question | Strong next book/chapter grouping; retain the three arguments because finite reversible chains, refreshed affine collisions and mass-composition closure have distinct hypotheses |
| A03 + A05 + A06 | Cut refinement, physical intervention and memory | A coherent future part; retain Gaussian restriction, collision/speed-support and telegraph conditioning proofs as distinct constructions |
| A07 | Controlled smooth reversal and sharp acceleration estimate | Keep as the mechanical comparison for impulsive A05/A06; its path class and cost differ |
| A09b | Coherent amplitudes, wavepacket limit and repeated measurement | Keep the quantum-rule comparison distinct from classical telegraph probability |
| A04 | Spatial-clock diagnostic | Pending task, with no accepted micropaper to merge |

These are real consolidation opportunities. The current cleanup takes the
clear same-model duplication first; a later thematic volume can reuse the
remaining proofs with shared notation. Similar subject matter alone is not
grounds for deleting a theorem or its assumptions.
