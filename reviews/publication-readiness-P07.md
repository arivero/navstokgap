## Editorial changes and consistency review

The gas introduction now states the matched-rate contribution and required
tools before the mechanics. The tagging paragraph defines stationary velocity
and stationary displacement increments separately from pinned position.
The conclusion identifies the **complete** collision interval, avoiding an
ambiguity with the initial residual interval, whose ordered mean is half as
large. The existing figure, comparison table and three classroom questions
already give a coherent teaching sequence; another model variant is unnecessary.

The spin introduction now gives the retained data explicitly as three means
per spin and nine cross moments. This is the existing normalized two-spin
tensor: its constant coordinate carries normalization, leaving fifteen varying
coordinates. The introduction also separates the explicit counterexample from
the linear-algebra closure proof. A new opening paragraph to Section 4 explains
the two propositions' roles before introducing the pullback notation.
