# R30 review: global recovery on a fixed preparation neighbourhood

C116–C117 are accepted as checked derivations in
[R30](../notes/three-calibration-global-recovery.md), with the reduced-box
hypothesis retained explicitly.

1. Analytic observability ensures that a third pulse can detect the R29
   kernel line. Positive fixed widths preserve four-row rank. The nonzero
   calibrated c supplies the energy row; no receiver-energy row is used.
2. The free-shear chart solves eta=a-lambda R by a uniform self-mapping
   contraction on an enlarged apparatus ball. All admitted record segments
   and counterfactual receiver segments stay in the chart domain, although
   a counterfactual apparatus need not satisfy the preparation constraints.
3. Subtraction of C(a) makes the divided numerator vanish for every w,a at
   zero coupling. Integral division gives joint smoothness, including the
   record derivatives needed for the noise bound. Dividing C-C_* directly
   would not establish this property for arbitrary records.
4. At nominal a the first-order pointer drift gives -K A_j/M_j; the clock
   impulse and integration by parts give -Kc B_1. Probe kinetic energy has
   no linear variation at the centre. The resulting derivative is constant
   over the whole receiver ball, so compactness permits a single preparation
   neighbourhood and coupling interval with a strict preconditioned margin.
5. Receiver convexity permits segment integration. Equal records and equal
   C imply equal receiver states, then equal apparatus states. This proves
   global injectivity on the specified class, beyond pointwise rank.
6. For differing records the C(a) subtraction contributes M_C/lambda.
   The smooth divided remainder contributes bounded M_D. Combining both
   with the inverse margin gives (8). A minimum-residual fit over the compact
   admitted class gives the factor two in each coordinate and factor four
   in the physical action-unit product (9).

The apparatus radius is chosen after c and the pulse design, then held fixed
as record error shrinks. No recovery on R29's full original box, sharp noise
rate, or uniform c-to-zero statement is accepted. Exact calibration and
apparatus-energy access are premises for R31 to test. The result advances the
exclusion-of-zero countertest; universality and quantum identification remain
open. [B61](../references/batches/B61.md) separates source support from these
model proofs. No numerical or symbolic verification scripts ran.
