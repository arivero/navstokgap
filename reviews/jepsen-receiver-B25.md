# A12/B25 review: a spatial collision clock

C050 realizes the A01 telegraph law through a prepared classical gas. The
proof is in the existing collision paper, section “A spatial receiver with an
exact collision clock”; this extends that manuscript rather than adding a
micropaper. Independent Poisson gaps in two thinned streams, divided by the
relative speed $2u$, give independent exponential waits of rate $\rho u$.

## Written checks

1. Equal masses exchange velocities, conserving both momentum and energy;
   the entire gas retains speeds $\pm u$.
2. The tag initially at $+u$ alternates through free lines with intercepts
   $B_j$ and $-A_j$. Ordered intercepts give the displayed collision times;
   their increments use disjoint independent gaps.
3. Bounded speed and local Poisson finiteness give finitely many relevant
   free lines on every finite window. Coincident multiple crossings have
   probability zero under continuous spacings.
4. The uniform initial sign makes the derived velocity chain stationary.
   Stationarity here concerns velocity and increments, not absolute tagged
   position fixed initially at zero. The untagged homogeneous marked gas has
   an invariant law under free translation and elastic label exchange.
5. The integrated covariance gives $H_*=mu/\rho$, with action units. Cooling
   at fixed density scales both the emergent rate and plateau linearly.
6. The infinite reservoir is defined first. Finite free-boundary gas at long
   times is a different limit. Finite density and finite energy density
   coexist with an infinite total reservoir energy.
