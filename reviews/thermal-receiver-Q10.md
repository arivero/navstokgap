2026-09-14. Coordinator review of the exploratory effective model; this is not
independent acceptance review or a bounded literature comparison.

- Probability signs: dark error is 1-exp(-r_0 tau); miss error conditional on
  an armed receiver is exp(-r_1 t_g). Thus both logarithmic inequalities in
  (4) have the stated directions. Joint success retains the survival factor.
- Rate cap: nu t_g<c is infeasible for every b and u. At equality D=0,
  detection requires u>=b and saturates its limit; no negative barrier is used.
- Optimization: b>=B and u>=max(0,b-D) are necessary and sufficient. The
  latter increases with b, so b=B minimizes it. The separate nu tau<=a
  branch makes both expressions in (7) zero under signal feasibility.
- Positive-bound case: c tau>a t_g and nu t_g>=c imply nu tau>a. Taking
  b=B and u=B-D gives dark rate a/tau and signal rate c/t_g exactly.
- Physical scope: a static initial point near the Q09 saddle is not a
  metastable stationary preparation. The text requires a mechanism before
  identifying its stored energy with a sustainable rate-model preload.
- The prefactor counterexample changes a model premise and does not prove
  zero physical control work. The work inequality explicitly uses u<=chi W;
  lowering a barrier is not automatically heat or reset expenditure.
- Units and limits: b,u,k_B T,W are energies; nu and r_i are rates; the log
  ratios are dimensionless. W/omega and W t_g have action units but differ.
  The cooling family is internal to the effective model, not a justified
  microscopic low-temperature limit.
