## Written checks

The normalized ball has affine dimension three and linear cone dimension four;
capacity two is a different quantity. Product tests determine all tensor
coordinates but only the minimal composite is admitted. The parity face has
two extreme states, so local continuity alone leaves the subspace premise open.

Hamiltonian signs agree with dot f={f,H}. Both selected preparations have
u_z=0, fixed second spin and identically zero energy. The product effect gives
(1-sin(Jt/S_A))/4 versus 1/4. Evolved moments still represent valid separable
states; the failure is an update on initial equivalence classes.

The finite-repair proof uses pointwise bounded Borel functions, not equivalence
modulo a fixed measure. Only the explicit exponentials are differentiated.
Distinct times give a nonsingular Vandermonde matrix for J/S_A nonzero.
The real atomic-separation lemma includes the constant function, ensuring
equal positive and negative masses. It covers nonlinear expectation updates
under all preparations, not arbitrary encodings lacking probabilistic mixing.

## Editorial and literature status

Restricted classical models and finite invariant-observable spaces are
established frameworks. B72/B74 supply bounded method coverage, not an
exhaustive search for this finished spin example. The manuscript makes no
priority claim. Its self-contained propositions reuse accepted proofs; the
reconstruction theorem is cited as context rather than reproved or presented
as a consequence of mechanical reversibility.

Before submission, perform one result-specific prior-art and positioning audit
of the combined example, then author review. The physical question concerns a
justification of the effect/preparation restrictions stable under interaction
and the separate time/energy-to-action identification. Another truncation
variant leaves that question unchanged.
