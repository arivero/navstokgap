\section{Gauge refinement: exact steps, scale dependence and the
infrared
obligations}\label{gauge-refinement-exact-steps-scale-dependence-and-the-infrared-obligations}}

\textbf{Maintained scope.} Series convolution is exact; transverse
elimination produces an effective interaction that must be retained and
controlled. Refining a lattice adds ultraviolet modes. A physical mass
gap and confinement are separate infrared claims: respectively a
spectral bound and an area law. The estimates below organize the proof
dependencies; no four-dimensional continuum gap is proved by this map.

One-loop transmutation supplies a possible physical scale, not a
positive spectral coefficient. Normalization, volume uniformity,
generated interactions, continuum reconstruction and visibility to
physical observables remain explicit obligations. A coarse pushforward
does not determine its fine conditional laws. Statuses refer to the
linked written arguments or identified literature; internal model
reviews are not independent peer review or novelty evidence.

\hypertarget{exact-operations-and-arbitrary-cuts}{%
\subsection{1. Exact operations and arbitrary
cuts}\label{exact-operations-and-arbitrary-cuts}}

For spacing \(a_\mu\) in \(D\) Euclidean dimensions, use heat times
\(t_{\mu\nu}=\lambda_Da_\mu a_\nu/\prod_{\rho\ne\mu,\nu}a_\rho\) in the
small-field normalization of the
\href{series-parallel-gauge-refinement.md}{series/parallel theorem}.
Halving one direction produces:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Series:} a face containing that direction splits into two half
  heat times. Integrating its new edge convolves weights; character
  coefficients multiply. Heat kernels close exactly in every dimension.
\item
  \textbf{Parallel:} each transverse face acquires a copy at doubled
  heat time. The mid-plane integral is a \((D-1)\)-dimensional gauge
  factor \(\Psi\) with coarse-field-conditioned bridge edges. Pointwise
  products add logarithms, but heat-kernel closure has compact image
  defects.
\item
  \textbf{Count:} there are \(\binom{D-1}{2}\) parallel planes per
  directional halving. An isotropic step has \(D\) directional moves and
  engineering heat time \(t=\lambda_Da^{4-D}\), before running is
  included.
\end{itemize}

A cut at any \(s\in(0,1)\) convolves heat times \(st\) and \((1-s)t\).
Trapezoid dual lengths keep transverse copies at heat time \(2t\); the
bridge variance is \(s(1-s)t_e\), and the free parallel defect is
multiplied by \(4s(1-s)\). The
\href{series-parallel-gauge-refinement.md}{series/parallel note},
Corollaries \(2_s\) and \(5_s\), gives the exact free calculation and
its \(U(1)\) extension. A cut schedule's physical scale change includes
both daughters and their dual volumes; assigning \(\log(1/s)\) to one
daughter alone loses that information.

For compact \(U(1)\) the bridge is a positive winding mixture; weights
are \(s\)-independent and shifts \(2\pi(1-s)W\) give \(\mathbb Z_q\) at
\(s=p/q\). For simply connected compact groups the
\href{sun-midpoint-centre.md}{exact character formula} is a signed
Weyl-polynomial Cartan-image expansion, not a proved positive mixture of
homotopy sectors. At a rational cut \(p/q\), the reached subgroup of the
\(SU(N)\) centre is \(\mathbb Z_{\gcd(q,N)}\). Thus dyadic cuts see the
\(SU(2)\) sign but only the identity in the \(SU(3)\) centre; trisection
sees triality. A central mid-edge flip creates thin flux on incident
faces. Whether those image terms organize physical centre vortices
remains open.

Newton's insertion is the series analogue. One cut spends
\(3s(1-s)K_\tau\), \(K_\tau=F^2\tau^3/(24m)\); complete mesh refinement
exhausts the cell correction. Its \href{cut-measure-newton.md}{cut
geometry} proves the additive shares and conditional Gaussian record
information. This analogy imports no gauge gap.

\hypertarget{the-circle-rotor-and-a-zero-dimensional-integral}{%
\subsubsection{The circle rotor and a zero-dimensional
integral}\label{the-circle-rotor-and-a-zero-dimensional-integral}}

The exact \(1+1\) circle spectrum \(E_R=\hbar c\lambda_2LC_2(R)/2\)
equals \(\hbar^2C_2(R)/(2I)\) with \(I=1/(cg_{\rm cl}^2L)\), an inertia
independent of \(\hbar\) at fixed classical coupling. The infinite-line
theory instead has no local particle; its static-charge string tension
is \(\hbar c\lambda_2C_2/2\). The
\href{zero-spacing-any-action.md}{zero-spacing theorem} fixes the
conventions.

A \(0+0\) integral has no Hamiltonian time evolution. Its large-\(N\)
transition is a limit in matrix size, not a continuum particle gap. For
a stable quartic integral, asymptotic perturbation theory alone does not
establish failure of Borel summation; the earlier unqualified claim of
non-Borel-summability is withdrawn. No such claim is needed for the
refinement argument.

\hypertarget{why-ultraviolet-refinement-bears-on-an-infrared-gap}{%
\subsection{3. Why ultraviolet refinement bears on an infrared
gap}\label{why-ultraviolet-refinement-bears-on-an-infrared-gap}}

Let \(\xi=\hbar/(mc)\) be a putative physical correlation length and
\(L\) the box size. A continuum limit sends \(a/\xi\to0\) at fixed
\(\xi/L\), followed by a separate infinite-volume limit when required.
The lattice gap is \(\delta=aE/(\hbar c)\); for \(E=mc^2\) it is
\(a/\xi\). A physical string tension has a different dimensionless
combination \(a^2\sigma/(\hbar c)\). A halving halves the former and
quarters the latter if the physical quantities survive. It does not
calculate their positive coefficients.

For \(D=4\), write \(t=\hbar g_{\rm cl}^2\) and \(b_0=11N/(48\pi^2)\).
At one loop, \[
 \mu\frac{dt}{d\mu}=-2b_0t^2+O(t^3),\qquad
 \Lambda=\mu e^{-1/(2b_0t(\mu))}.
\] The displayed \(\Lambda\) relation uses the one-loop truncation;
higher orders add prefactors and scheme dependence. A measured coupling
fixes one normalization of \(\Lambda\). A scheme change rescales it and
the numbers \(C_i\) inversely so that a putative
\(E_i=C_i\hbar c\Lambda\) is invariant. Neither running nor dimensional
analysis proves \(C_i>0\).

\hypertarget{exponential-corrections-and-the-spectral-coefficient}{%
\subsubsection{Exponential corrections and the spectral
coefficient}\label{exponential-corrections-and-the-spectral-coefficient}}

Within the one-loop relation, \[e^{-\chi/t}=(a\Lambda)^{2b_0\chi}.\] A
dimension-one lattice gap would have exponent \(2b_0\chi=1\). An image
term with exponent greater than four can be suppressed even after
counting \(O((L/a)^4)\) cells, provided its normalized boundary-uniform
bound is proved. Root-image exponent \(\chi=8\pi^2\) gives \(11N/3\);
the \(SU(3)\) trisection centre exponent \(24\pi^2\) gives \(33\). The
dislocation criterion \(\chi>2/b_0\) is \(6/11\) of the instanton action
for \(SU(2)\). These powers are a formal scaling diagnostic for the
stated image exponents, not uniform estimates for every large field. The
\href{zero-spacing-any-action.md}{zero-spacing treatment} and
\href{sun-midpoint-centre.md}{centre calculation} preserve their precise
scopes.

Exponentially small terms have vanishing Taylor coefficients at zero
coupling; a perturbative power series cannot establish their positive
spectral weight. The earlier analogy to renormalon positions was sourced
only to metadata and memory and supplies no identification of these
image terms with a renormalon. It is not used as evidence.

Below four dimensions \(\lambda_D=\hbar g_{\rm cl}^2\) carries length
power \(D-4\), and dimensional energy is \(\hbar c\lambda_D^{1/(4-D)}\).
This scales as \(\hbar^{3/2}\) in \(1+1\) and \(\hbar^2\) in \(1+2\) at
fixed classical coupling. At \(D=4\) the running equation supplies an
exponential scale instead of this dimensional power. This is an RG
statement, not a derivation by formally setting \(D=4\) in the divergent
exponent. The rotor and quartic matrix model have their own \(\hbar^2\)
and \(\hbar^{4/3}\) laws, proved in the
\href{low-dimensional-mass-gap.md}{low-dimensional comparison}.

\hypertarget{two-uniformities-and-a-constructed-measure}{%
\subsubsection{Two uniformities and a constructed
measure}\label{two-uniformities-and-a-constructed-measure}}

The number of steps between \(a\) and a fixed \(\xi\) grows as
\(\log_2(\xi/a)\). Refinement adds weak-coupling steps at the
ultraviolet end. A fixed range of physical scales near \(\xi\) still
requires infrared control. The
\href{confinement-scale-bands.md}{confinement-scale bands} collect the
finite-lattice Wilson-action evidence; numerical bands or a finite
verification proposal are not a certified mixing theorem.

Uniformity in refinement depth as \(a\to0\) and uniformity in box size
as \(L\to\infty\) are separate. If \(p_*\) is the coarse pushforward,
then \(\langle F\rangle_{p_*\mu}=\langle F\circ p\rangle_\mu\) exactly,
but many fine conditional measures have that same coarse law. A
compatible refinement family must be constructed. Transport of a
coarse-loop channel also requires spectral visibility to reach a gap for
all relevant physical observables. Continuum nontriviality and
reconstruction remain further steps.

\hypertarget{supplied-action-lattice-gaps-and-physical-limits}{%
\subsection{4. Supplied action, lattice gaps and physical
limits}\label{supplied-action-lattice-gaps-and-physical-limits}}

Compact \(U(1)\) in \(1+2\) has a lattice monopole gap that vanishes in
the fixed-\(\lambda_3\) continuum limit (Gross). Along the Göpfert--Mack
scaling \(\lambda_3a\simeq c_0/[2\log(1/a)]\), \(c_0\simeq4.99\), a
massive Gaussian limit instead survives with divergent physical monopole
density. The \href{villain-monopole-refinement.md}{monopole note} gives
the source and trajectory qualifications. A lattice gap alone is thus
insufficient.

Physical \(\hbar\) is supplied in both Gaussian theories; it is not an
independently derived lower bound on every action difference. Removing a
mass gap need not remove quantum kinematics. Conversely a generated
scale need not imply a mass gap: massless QCD under spontaneous chiral
symmetry breaking has pions, and an infrared fixed point has gapless
infrared behavior. The two-dimensional delta potential has a transmuted
binding energy and a continuum above zero; its bound-state energy is not
a field-theory mass-gap proof.
\href{low-dimensional-mass-gap.md}{Low-dimensional models} and the
\href{action-floor-yang-mills-gap.md}{action/gap comparison} retain
these distinct mechanisms. Confinement and a physical spectral gap each
require their own argument.

\hypertarget{remaining-proof-obligations}{%
\subsection{5. Remaining proof
obligations}\label{remaining-proof-obligations}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Nonabelian spatial blocking:} extend the retained-barrier
  estimates (112), (116) and centered quadratic source test (177) to the
  full nonlinear source bound (113), physical recoupling, contact
  cancellation, target-covering paths and perturbed-action iteration.
  The \href{su2-midplane-small-field.md}{small-field notebook} holds the
  constants and proofs; its individual stages are not independent
  confirmations of the target theorem.
\item
  \textbf{Four-dimensional depth:} control generated cubic/quartic
  kernels, subtracted contractions, endpoint matching and the
  accumulated remainder. The conditional composition theorem gives the
  averaged \(-2b_0\log2\) coefficient only under those hypotheses.
  \href{four-dimensional-parallel-log.md}{One-step parallel matching}
  and \href{four-dimensional-composition.md}{Schur composition} prove
  different parts.
\item
  \textbf{Time-only halving:} at fixed spatial lattice, electric heat
  kernels compose in series and exponential magnetic weights add in
  parallel. Their noncommutation is controlled in the strong Trotter
  limit for the compact-group Laplacian plus bounded magnetic potential
  (Trotter 1959; Chernoff 1968; Kogut--Susskind 1975 and Creutz 1977,
  metadata in the source companions). A norm rate needs separate
  commutator domains. Spatial continuum uniformity does not follow from
  this fixed-lattice Hamiltonian construction.
\item
  \textbf{Cut schedules:} free-field shape convergence and nonabelian
  generated weights require control beyond the one-cut factor
  \(4s(1-s)\). Whether triadic \(SU(3)\) refinement helps organize large
  fields is open.
\item
  \textbf{Records in the mechanical analogy:}
  \href{mark-cost-and-statistical-floor.md}{Gaussian marks} and
  \href{sed-closure-under-recording.md}{complete controllers} retain
  conditional noise, conjugate recoil and weighted covariance budgets. A
  total precision sum alone does not characterize arbitrary changing
  triangular arrays. The \href{score-constrained-ensemble.md}{score
  model} passes its branch-dependent composition test at every supplied
  coefficient, including zero; composition alone selects no positive
  action.
\item
  \textbf{Infrared and reconstruction:} a nontrivial physical continuum
  measure, volume-uniform spectral positivity, observable visibility and
  an independent area-law argument remain necessary.
  \href{mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md\#existing-estimates-and-examined-routes}{Mass-gap
  position} and \href{mass-gap-obligations-lattice.md}{continuum
  obligations} state the targets.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{consequence-for-state}{%
\subsection{6. Consequence for STATE}\label{consequence-for-state}}

Exact refinement identities locate the missing estimates; they do not
replace them. Keep ultraviolet control, infrared spectral positivity,
confinement, construction of the continuum theory and the supplied
action unit separate. The linked arguments hold the proofs and source
evidence; this map supplies only their dependencies and scaling
conventions.
