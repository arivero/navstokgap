\begin{abstract}
A restartable programme for testing action-scale restrictions and gap formation
in elementary mechanics. It separates classical obstructions, conditional quantum
bounds, reconstruction hypotheses and future field-theory comparisons. This is
a research design, not a claim that quantum mechanics or a mass gap has been derived.
The maintained source is \texttt{research/PROGRAMME.md}; this LaTeX is generated
from it. Operational task state is kept separately in the repository.
\end{abstract}
